# Worked Examples

A full daily-scope brainstorm run for a **grass-cutting Roblox simulator** (the game discussed when this skill was created). Demonstrates the ranked output format, exclusivity tagging (exclusive vs. `[generic]`), hybrid tags, monetization honesty, and how a run surfaces cross-scope connectors.

Game context used for this run: core loop = unbox weapons with cash → cut grass → sell grass for cash; weapons and grass are color-typed (red/blue/white) with matchup bonuses; tiered grass zones; quest-leveling for equip slots; planned clans/trading/leaderboards; young audience; aggressive monetization; no rebirth.

Ideas are ranked best-fit-first. The most thematically-exclusive ideas rank highest; generic-but-effective ideas are ranked lower and honestly tagged.

---

### 1. Weather System
**Concept:** Each real-world day has in-game weather (sunny / rain / drought / frost / storm) that shifts grass values and growth rates. Players log in to check the forecast and optimize what they farm today.
**Why it fits this scope (daily):** Resets every 24 hours — "what's the weather today?" becomes a daily ritual.
**Why it's exclusive:** Only works because grass is color-typed. Weather maps to grass colors (rain → blue grass thrives, drought → red grass spikes, frost → white grass drops bonus essence). A generic sim with one resource has nothing for weather to act on.
**Strengths:** Native to the grass/lawn theme; rewards weapon diversity (red weapons on drought day, blue on rain day); cheap to build (multiplier tables); creates a natural event calendar.
**Risks:** Balancing — if one weather is clearly best, other days feel like "bad days"; younger kids may need a tutorial to grasp why weather matters.
**Hybrid tags:** also touches: mid-term (session planning around the day's weather).
**Monetization:** Weather Report reroll (peek tomorrow's rates early), Weather Shield (lock favorable weather an extra day), Controlled Climate gamepass (small personal weather bonus regardless of world weather).

### 2. Auto-Mower
**Concept:** Deploy a robotic lawnmower on a grass patch; it auto-harvests while you're offline up to 8 hours and deposits the grass on your next login. Upgradable for speed, capacity, and higher-tier zone access.
**Why it fits this scope (daily):** 8-hour offline timer maps to "set it before school, collect after."
**Why it's exclusive:** A robotic mower collecting grass is inseparable from the grass-cutting theme. No other sim genre can do this naturally.
**Strengths:** Thematically flawless; "how much did my mower collect?" is a genuine login pull; parallel upgrade track; zero tutorial needed (kids know what a mower does).
**Risks:** If too strong, it replaces active cutting; needs server-side offline simulation (technical cost).
**Hybrid tags:** also touches: mid-term (mower upgrade grind).
**Monetization:** Extra mower slots, premium fuel (longer runtime + better yield), cosmetic mower skins, Lucky Mower gamepass (chance to harvest rare grass types).

### 3. Garden Plot
**Concept:** A personal garden where you plant grass seeds (rare drops from cutting); seeds grow over 4–8 real hours into premium harvestable grass. Watering (free, every 4 hours) speeds growth.
**Why it fits this scope (daily):** Multi-hour grow timers + a watering cadence = daily check-ins. "Plant before bed, harvest on wake" is a perfect ritual.
**Why it's exclusive:** Growing grass from seeds is the literal inverse of the core loop (cutting grass) — only a grass game can make planting the retention hook.
**Strengths:** Combines idle (grows offline) with planning/strategy; seed rarity creates a secondary collection goal; natural bedtime ritual.
**Risks:** Multiple timer mechanics (Investor + Mower + Garden) may clutter the UI; over-monetization turns it into pay-to-win farming.
**Hybrid tags:** also touches: mid-term (planting/harvesting is a mini-session).
**Monetization:** Extra garden plots, premium seeds, Auto-Sprinkler (auto-waters), Miracle-Gro instant-growth consumable, exclusive seed types.

### 4. Weed Bounty
**Concept:** 1–3 "boss weeds" spawn daily in designated areas with high HP and color-specific weaknesses; defeating them drops rare loot (exclusive cosmetics, large cash, crafting essences, pet eggs).
**Why it fits this scope (daily):** Rotating daily targets — "what's today's bounty?" is a strong daily pull.
**Why it's exclusive:** Boss weeds requiring color-matched weapons only works because of the color matchup system. A generic sim's "daily boss" is portable; a color-locked weed is not.
**Strengths:** Thematically perfect; adds non-violent combat (shields, regen, minion weeds); group bounties tie into clans; viral/TikTok potential.
**Risks:** Boss design/animation work; too hard frustrates young kids, too easy bores veterans.
**Hybrid tags:** also touches: mid-term (the fight itself), long-term (rare-drop collection).
**Monetization:** Bounty Hunter gamepass (extra daily bounty slot), bounty reroll, Bounty Radar consumable (reveals tomorrow's target early).

### 5. Lawn of the Day
**Concept:** A shared mega-lawn opens for 1 hour daily; players compete to harvest the most grass from it. Live leaderboard; top 100 get rewards, all participants get a participation prize. Solo or clan-based.
**Why it fits this scope (daily):** A time-gated 1-hour daily window creates urgency and a daily ritual.
**Why it's exclusive:** A competitive grass-harvesting event is only possible in a grass-cutting game.
**Strengths:** Pulls competitive up-market PC players daily; clan version = social retention; leaderboard bragging rights are permanent free marketing.
**Risks:** Server architecture for mass participation; down-market casuals may never engage.
**Hybrid tags:** also touches: long-term (leaderboard/competition grind).
**Monetization:** Extra entries, Golden Scythe consumable (2x harvest rate in the event), VIP leaderboard tier.

### 6. Grass-Fuel Machine
**Concept:** A hub-area machine runs on grass donations from all players. When the global meter hits 100%, it activates for 1 hour (doubled grass values, doubled drop rates, special enemies spawn).
**Why it fits this scope (daily):** "Did the machine activate today?" becomes a daily community question.
**Why it's exclusive:** Grass as fuel for a community machine only makes sense inside a grass economy.
**Strengths:** Community cooperation is inherently viral; creates spontaneous "server parties"; daily social pull.
**Risks:** Needs a large active player base or the meter stalls; free-rider problem (people wait for others to fill it).
**Hybrid tags:** also touches: long-term (community/clan dynamics).
**Monetization:** Overcharge token (your contribution counts 5x toward the meter), personal boost during activation.

### 7. Mystery Seed
**Concept:** One free mystery seed per daily login. Plant it, wait 24 hours, it grows into a random reward — common grass, a rare cosmetic plant, a pet egg, a weapon, or (rarely) a legendary item.
**Why it fits this scope (daily):** Combines a daily login + a 24-hour timer anticipation in one mechanic.
**Why it's exclusive:** Seeds growing into rewards is grass-theme-native; the "what grew?" reveal is a grass-game ritual.
**Strengths:** Login + timer anticipation combined; the mystery/gacha element is inherently exciting; simple to implement.
**Risks:** 24 hours is long for impatient kids (but that's the retention point); random outcomes can disappoint.
**Hybrid tags:** also touches: long-term (legendary chase).
**Monetization:** Extra daily seeds, Blessed Seed (guaranteed rare+ outcome), faster growth.

### 8. Grass Donation NPC
**Concept:** Once per day an NPC requests a specific grass donation ("I need 100 red grass for my science experiment"). Donating builds reputation; at thresholds the NPC grants permanent rewards (exclusive weapon, pet, cosmetic, passive bonus). Different NPCs rotate daily.
**Why it fits this scope (daily):** One donation per day = a daily check-in with a relationship progression underneath.
**Why it's exclusive:** Grass as the donation currency ties it to the core loop; "the NPC needs grass" only makes sense in a grass game.
**Strengths:** Feels like helping (works well for young kids); reputation is a long-tail grind with daily touchpoints; rotating NPCs add personality/variety.
**Risks:** NPC variety and writing demand; kids may find it boring vs. combat/collection.
**Hybrid tags:** also touches: long-term (reputation grind).
**Monetization:** Reputation boosters, NPC Favor (summon a specific NPC).

### 9. Composter
**Concept:** Dump unwanted grass, low-tier weapons, or junk items into a composter. After 4–6 hours it produces fertilizer that temporarily boosts grass growth rate, grass value, or weapon damage. Input quality determines output quality.
**Why it fits this scope (daily):** Multi-hour timer = set-and-return; fertilizer is a "use it when you log in" consumable that creates a gameplay beat on login.
**Why it's exclusive [semi]:** Grass→fertilizer is theme-native, but "convert junk → buff" exists in other sims. The grass framing is exclusive; the mechanic shape is portable.
**Strengths:** Gives value to trash items that would otherwise feel like clutter; fertilizer as a consumable creates a "use it now" loop on login; simple risk-free timer.
**Risks:** Less exciting than the Investor (no mystery/surprise); kids may not intuitively grasp composting.
**Hybrid tags:** also touches: short-term (fertilizer use is a quick gameplay beat).
**Monetization:** Extra compost bins, Super Compost speed-up, premium compost recipes (guaranteed rare fertilizer).

### 10. Weapon Crafting Station
**Concept:** Combine grass + cash + essence (a rare drop from cutting grass) to craft weapon crates over 4–6 hours. Different recipes produce different rarity crates; recipes unlock via quests. Higher-tier zones drop essences for better recipes.
**Why it fits this scope (daily):** Multi-hour craft timers + a queue = daily return to collect and re-queue.
**Why it's exclusive [semi]:** Grass + essence as crafting inputs ties it to the core loop, but "craft on a timer" is portable. The grass-input framing is exclusive; the mechanic shape is generic.
**Strengths:** Deepens the core loop (grass isn't just for selling anymore); recipe discovery adds a collection/logbook meta; essence as a rare drop makes every cut a tiny gamble.
**Risks:** Complexity may frustrate younger kids; too many parallel timers = chore; must not replace weapon unboxing (economy tuning).
**Hybrid tags:** also touches: mid-term (recipe collection), long-term (best weapons).
**Monetization:** Speed-up timers, extra crafting queue slots, premium recipes (Robux-only), Master Crafter gamepass (shorter timers permanently).

### 11. Weapon Investor
**Concept:** Place weapons into an NPC rental stand; the NPC rents them out to other NPCs for 6 hours; you earn cash + grass interest based on the weapon's damage, rarity, and color type. A red weapon might earn more on days when red grass is in demand.
**Why it fits this scope (daily):** 6-hour timer maps to "before school → after school" or "lunch → evening."
**Why it's exclusive [generic]:** The deposit-and-wait shape is portable — any sim can do "stash item, wait, get reward." The weapon-rental + color-demand framing adds some exclusivity, but the core mechanic is generic. Honestly tagged.
**Strengths:** Proven sticky formula (Pet Daycare works); encourages collecting diverse weapons, not just the strongest; feeds directly into the core loop.
**Risks:** Generic — any sim can clone this; tuning tightrope (too low = ignored, too high = replaces active play); kids need clear UI to understand why a weak red weapon sometimes beats a strong blue one.
**Hybrid tags:** also touches: mid-term (weapon loadout planning).
**Monetization:** Additional investor slots (Robux), instant-claim speed-up, VIP investor (better interest rates).

### 12. Daily Quest Chain
**Concept:** 3–5 daily quests forming a chain (cut 200 grass → cut 100 red grass with a red weapon → sell 500 grass → bonus for completing all). Full-completion streak bonuses stack across days.
**Why it fits this scope (daily):** Resets daily; the chain structure extends the daily session beyond a single quest.
**Why it's exclusive [generic]:** Quest chains are portable. The grass/weapon-color quest targets add theme, but the shape is generic. Honestly tagged.
**Strengths:** Gives direction to "what do I do today?"; chain structure keeps players in-session longer; streak bonus adds a second retention layer.
**Risks:** Can feel like a chore list if too grindy; quest variety is hard to sustain (dev overhead).
**Hybrid tags:** also touches: short-term (the quests themselves are short play arcs).
**Monetization:** Quest reroll, extra daily quest slot, auto-complete a quest.

### 13. Daily Quests (individual)
**Concept:** A small set of standalone daily quests (e.g., "cut 500 grass of any type," "use a blue weapon 50 times"), each granting a small reward. Reset every 24 hours.
**Why it fits this scope (daily):** Per-day reset; quick directed goals.
**Why it's exclusive [generic]:** Portable mechanic; theme-able but not exclusive. Honestly tagged.
**Strengths:** Dead simple; low dev cost; gives casuals a reason to play with direction.
**Risks:** Less engaging than the chain variant; can feel like a checklist.
**Hybrid tags:** also touches: short-term.
**Monetization:** Quest reroll, extra quest slot.

### 14. Streak Calendar
**Concept:** A 7-day or 30-day login calendar with escalating rewards (Day 1 = small cash, Day 7 = rare weapon crate, Day 30 = exclusive cosmetic weapon). Miss a day = streak resets (or drops one tier).
**Why it fits this scope (daily):** Pure login-streak mechanic.
**Why it's exclusive [generic]:** Every mobile game has this — zero differentiation. Honestly tagged.
**Strengths:** Dead simple (8-year-olds understand it); extremely low dev cost; guaranteed baseline retention lift; seasonal/event calendars keep it fresh.
**Risks:** Zero differentiation; drives login, not play; creates entitlement when the calendar "ends."
**Hybrid tags:** also touches: long-term (30-day reward tier).
**Monetization:** Streak saver (Robux), buy-back missed days, premium reward track with better payouts.

### 15. Daily Rewards
**Concept:** A simple per-day login reward (cash, grass, occasional crate) with no streak escalation — just "show up, get a thing."
**Why it fits this scope (daily):** Pure per-day login hook.
**Why it's exclusive [generic]:** Fully portable. Honestly tagged.
**Strengths:** Simplest possible retention hook; near-zero dev cost.
**Risks:** Weakest retention hook on its own; entitlement risk.
**Hybrid tags:** —
**Monetization:** Premium track, doubled-reward gamepass.

### 16. Daily Spin Wheel
**Concept:** One free daily spin on a visible prize wheel. Prizes: cash, grass, essences, temporary boosts, rare cosmetics, exclusive pets.
**Why it fits this scope (daily):** One free spin per day.
**Why it's exclusive [generic]:** Portable; the wheel itself has no grass-theme link unless prizes do. Honestly tagged.
**Strengths:** Viscerally satisfying (kids love spinning wheels); the animation is a dopamine hit; tiered prizes mirror the unboxing joy.
**Risks:** Gambling-adjacent (may draw parental scrutiny); prize pool needs constant refreshing or it goes stale.
**Hybrid tags:** —
**Monetization:** Extra spins, premium wheel (better prizes), spin tokens in the cash shop.

### 17. Pet Egg Incubation
**Concept:** Eggs obtained from cutting grass and completing quests hatch over 4–12 real hours (rarity-dependent). Up to 3 incubator slots.
**Why it fits this scope (daily):** Multi-hour hatch timers = return-to-collect loop.
**Why it's exclusive [generic]:** Egg hatching is portable across any sim. Honestly tagged. (The grass-drop acquisition adds slight theme, but the mechanic is generic.)
**Strengths:** Tried-and-true (Pokemon Go, Pet Sim, Adopt Me all use this); "what's inside?" anticipation is a strong emotional pull; fits the 1–3 pet complementary scope.
**Risks:** Only works if pets are in the game; fully generic.
**Hybrid tags:** also touches: long-term (pet collection).
**Monetization:** Extra incubator slots, instant hatch, Blessed Incubator (better pet odds).

### 18. Seasonal Battle Pass — *cross-scope connector*
**Concept:** A 30-day season with a free track of 20–30 tiers. Earn XP by completing daily quests, cutting grass, defeating weed bounties, and contributing to clan goals. Rewards: cash, crates, exclusive seasonal weapons, cosmetics, pet eggs, emotes.
**Why it fits this scope (daily) — as a connector, not a daily idea:** Daily systems (quests, bounties, weather) are what feed the pass's XP. The pass is the structure that gives daily play a long-term payoff.
**Why it's exclusive [generic]:** Battle passes are portable. Honestly tagged. (Grass-themed seasonal rewards add flavor, but the shape is generic.)
**Note:** This is **not** a daily-scope idea — its true primary scope is **long-term** (a 30-day season). It's included here to demonstrate how a daily run surfaces cross-scope connectors: the daily systems above are what make the pass progress, so the pass naturally appears as context when brainstorming daily systems that need a long-term payoff.
**Strengths:** Industry-standard for a reason; ties all daily systems into one rewards track; the visual progress bar is satisfying; the free track alone is a strong daily driver for non-spenders.
**Risks:** High content demand (new seasonal rewards every month); the premium track needs enough value to justify purchase without gutting the free track.
**Hybrid tags:** primary: long-term; also touches: daily (XP source), weekly (season cadence).
**Monetization:** Premium track (Robux), tier skips, Season Pass Plus (starts at tier 10 + bonus XP).
