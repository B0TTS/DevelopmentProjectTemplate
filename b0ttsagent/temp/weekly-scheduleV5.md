## 💡 Insight Highlights

1. **Exact 8 / 8 / 8 Balance Mon–Sat** — every weekday hits 8.0h Work (POW), 8.0h Free (POF), and 8.0h Sleep. Sunday stays a 0h POW day off (16.0h POF / 8.0h Sleep).
2. **Friday is the Tightest POF Day** — with Morning Ritual (1.5h), Sunlight (1.0h), Breakfast (1.0h), Gym (2.0h), MC Hardcore Stream (2.0h), and Cleaning (0.17h), Friday has 7.67h of fixed POF. This leaves only **0.33h (~20 minutes)** of Extra Personal Freetime.
3. **Gaming as POW Integration** — Mobile Gaming (30m) and PC Gaming (30m) run Mon–Sat as fixed POW tasks (6.0h total/week), ensuring market research and Roblox game ideation are treated strictly as work obligations.
4. **Morning Ritual Expanded Container** — 1.5h daily Mon–Sat (9.0h/week). Manifestations/Affirmations run Mon/Wed/Sat (30m), adjusting internal teeth/prep distribution without altering the total container duration.
5. **Gym Days** — Go Gym runs 3x/week on **Tue, Fri, Sat** for 2.0h per session (6.0h total/week).
6. **Streaming Structure** — Streaming Normal runs Mon/Wed (2.0h each, POF). Streaming MC Hardcore runs Fri (required, 2.0h) and Sun (optional, 2.0h).
7. **Sunday Flexibility** — Sunday includes fixed anchors (Moral Code Review 15m, Breakfast 1h, Skate 1h) plus optional MC Hardcore stream (2h) and optional Clip Farm (3h), leaving 8.75h of completely unstructured Extra Personal Freetime.
