# Founder Profile

This document contains the deep personal profile used to generate business ideas. Every idea must connect back to multiple sections of this profile. If an idea doesn't touch at least 3 categories below, it's too generic — discard it.

- **Spiritual/philosophical**: Practices meditation daily (non-negotiable). Into spirituality, tarot, existentialism. Believes humanity is at a turning point with AI ("we either make god or make the devil").
- **Personality**: All-or-nothing. Steady grinder. Goes deep on one thing rather than spreading thin. Has a latent leadership magnetism that activates when built up. Loves everyone. Superpower is refusing to give up.
