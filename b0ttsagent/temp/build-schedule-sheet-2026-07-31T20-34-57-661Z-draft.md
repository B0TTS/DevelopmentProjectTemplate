## Legend and Accounting Rules

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **POW** | Part of Work — counts toward the daily work total |
| **POF** | Part of Freetime — counts toward the daily free total |
| **Sleep** | Separate category — sleep baseline |
| **NR** | Not Required — a recommendation only; 0 scheduled minutes; never enters any total |
| **Freeday exception** | Runs on the non-free days; skipped on the free day |
| **Flex** | Filled block — expands to hit the category budget (budget − fixed) |
| **Conditional** | Optional block — visible but not counted by default; draws down a flex block when taken |

Accounting: the free day (sun) is shown for reference and **excluded** from weekly totals, averages, percentages, and rankings. Counted days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday. Daily targets apply per the spec budgets.

## Task Details / Execution Notes

### 1. Morning Ritual *(POF, 1h 30m)*

**Days:** Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday

Morning ritual be op. Wake up 8-9am. 1.5h capped container; per-day variance in subtasks — uniform subtask-sum check is intentionally skipped.

**Subtasks:**

- Meditation — 30m — days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday
  - Sit idle, no distractions daily. Keeps mind clear, better meditation. Since it's at 8am, you can get your sunlight in easy pz.
- Manifestations / Affirmations — 30m — days: Monday, Wednesday, Saturday
- Clear out air per the guide — 15m
- Rest of hour: brush teeth, clean room, etc. — days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday
  - Flex remainder — fills whatever time is left in the 1.5h container after the other subtasks. Varies by day: ~15m on Mon/Wed/Sat, ~45m on Tue/Thu/Fri.
  - Clean Room
  - Brush Teeth
  - Do hair
  - Get oiled up

### 2. Sunlight *(POF, conditional (0–1h))*

**Days:** Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday

0–1h. Try to get 30m of sunlight (travel times so 1 hour max). Skip if enough sunlight was gotten during the 8am Morning Ritual. Conditional; not counted toward the 8h POF total by default; draws down Extra Personal Freetime when taken.

**Days:** Sunday

### 5. Weekly Review + Goals *(POW, 1h)*

**Days:** Saturday

**Subtasks:**

- Weekly Review — 30m
  - Ask yourself what worked, what didn't, and what needs to change for the past week. Journal or capture lessons and set priorities for the coming week. Why: reduced unfinished tasks, lowered after-work rumination, and improved cognitive flexibility.
- Week Goals / Plans — 30m
  - Plan out when you're going to do what throughout the week — skill studies, streams, etc. In case you miss adding a task in subtasks, this will help. Be sure to add missing subtasks!
  - Plan out skill study days
  - Plan out what days you will do streams, and what you will do for the streams

### 17. Streaming Normal (not fried) *(POF, 2h)*

**Days:** Monday, Wednesday, Saturday

Don't start streaming until you have a monetized MVP for Whack Grass. It doesn't have to be a bunch of content, just a bare minimum template. This will generate some income which is what you need ASAP. Don't worry about it growing or dying as you work on development. Just create something and push for the long term — you know it will work out.

**Subtasks:**

- Plan / prep / study — 30m
  - Spend 30m planning out, and/or studying, and getting ready for anything stream related.
- Stream — 1h 30m
  - Mask stream: make content, be engaging, conversate with people, practice conversational skills. Why: helps keep your conversational skills good, gives practice with being engaging, being an active listener, and a fun person to talk to.

### 18. Clip Farm *(POF, NR)*

**Days:** Sunday

NR. You don't gotta do this. Just lock in and make clips, and/or study content creation if you feel like it. Spend 2–3h max; however you can spend as much time as you feel. Recommended: split time into thirds — 1/3 studying content creation, 1/3 content creation for chill streams, 1/3 content creation for main streams. But honestly don't take it too seriously; if you wanna just only make content for a specific channel, do that. 0 scheduled minutes; comes out of Extra Personal Freetime if done.

### 19. Extra Personal Freetime *(POF, flex)*
