          · Organizational Transformation: We underwent a transformative, cost-effective restructuring to
              enhance our Agency's capabilities to achieve its registration mission and deliver an exceptional

              cloud-based platform, a streamlined effort scheduled for completion in 2025. Additionally, SSS

              enhance operational resilience, and ensure readiness to address future challenges.
