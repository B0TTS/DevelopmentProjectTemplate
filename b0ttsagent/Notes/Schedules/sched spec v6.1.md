# Layout V4

## How to do Planning for daily schedule
#### **Read First**
*   As you go through the steps, add things to your daily schedule, and make sure you have you monthly and weekly things scheduled and moved into whatever time category is needed.
*   Once your quick schedule is setup, start working on your obsidian daily schedule.
*   Write down more secret todo tasks on session log paper.
*   When you check your goals, make sure you take a look at the start date and reflect on your progress as well as possible needs for pivot strategies.
*   Make sure that you schedule the most draining and tiring things first, and the most dopaminergic things last.

**Steps:**
1. Read _scheduling tips_ under this list
2. Pull schedule tasks from _Stepped Schedule Layout_
3. Check all of your goals and Look at calendar
4. Check all of your todo tasks in Plans list
5. Check all of your check daily
6. Check all of your check weekly
7. Check all of your check monthly
8. Make sure your quick schedule is perfect. Once its perfect, continue to next step. These next steps are for obsidian sched.
9. Check your previous days session logs and missed tasks
10. Check do tomorrow (paper)
11. Check your backlog list
12. Check all of your goals again
13. Spend rest of time doing thinking time.

**Scheduling Tips**
*   Swallow the frog
    *   "Swallow the frog and everything's easier for the rest of the day"
    *   Do what's hardest first (dopamine, serotonin)
*   Micro and Macro Time blocking
*   **8 Hours of work, Free time, and Sleep per day**
*   Minimize multi tasking during deep work (no vibe coding boy!)
    *   **why:** Multitasking feels like progress but this fragmented approach actually impedes deep work and leads to reduced efficiency
*   **Track Everything**
    *   Track every single thing that you do with clockify.

## Tips and Information
#### Not Required (NR)
Not required basically means that a task is not required to be done. It is simply a reccomendation/reminder that said task would be best done when stated via the task.

#### Part of Work (POW)
Part of work basically means that a task is included inside of the total work time per day, and is not seperate.

#### Part of Freetime (POF)
Part of freetime means that a task is included with your total free time, which is around six to eight hours per day.
