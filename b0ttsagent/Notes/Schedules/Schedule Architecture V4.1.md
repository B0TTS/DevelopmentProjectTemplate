# Dev Focus Mode

## Tips and Information
#### Not Required (NR)
Not required basically means that a task is not required to be done. It is simply a reccomendation/reminder that said task would be best done when stated via the task.

#### Part of Work (POW)
Part of work basically means that a task is included inside of the total work time per day, and is not seperate.

#### Part of Freetime (POF)
Part of freetime means that a task is included with your total free time, which is around six to eight hours per day.
