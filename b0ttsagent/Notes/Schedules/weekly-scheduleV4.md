## 💡 Insight Highlights

1. **Perfectly balanced Mon–Sat** — every weekday hits exactly 8 / 8 / 8 (POW / POF / Sleep). Sunday is the controlled recovery valve (0 / 16 / 8).
2. **Only ~12.8% of your week is raw "flex Work"** — the bulk of POW is intentional deep-work blocks (Streaming Dev 9h, End of Day Ritual 6h, Skill Study 4.5h, Planning + Ideation 6h combined). Your "job" is mostly structured craft, not undifferentiated labor.
3. **Friday is your tightest day** — 6.5h of fixed POW leaves only 1.5h of flex Work. If anything runs over, Friday is the day it breaks.
4. **Saturday is your planning anchor** — only day with Weekly Review + Goals, MC Hardcore stream, AND dehumidifier cleaning stacked. Its flex Work (5h) is the cushion.
5. **Health/wellness investment** = Gym 6h + Meditation 3h + Morning Ritual 6h + Sunlight 3h + Skate 1h = **19h/week (~11% of waking hours)** — solid.
6. **Streaming totals 15h/week** (9h streaming dev is POW; 6h MC hardcore is POF). Streaming dev ≈ 3h planning/study + 6h streaming; MC hardcore ≈ 2h prep + 4h stream. You're **actually on-stream ~10h/week** with ~5h prep/study around it.
7. **Sunday's 13.75h unstructured free time** is intentional reset — but Moral Code Review + Skate are your only anchors. Consider whether you want at least one more anchor so the day doesn't drift.
8. **Flex Work is largest Tue/Thu (4.5h)** — those are your best days for big deep-work pushes that aren't pre-allocated.
