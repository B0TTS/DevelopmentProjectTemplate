## 2. Step-by-Step Workflow
Self-documented across 2018–2026 interviews and her own vlogs; the constant across eras is a first-line-first, solo-at-instrument process:

1. **First line first** — "I always start with a first line for the most part. I've had a few songs where I wrote the chorus first, but that's rare" ([Coup de Main, 2019](https://www.coupdemainmagazine.com/tessa-violet/15439)); "I start with one phrase, usually the first line of the first verse but occasionally the chorus. A lot of my songs are a spill of consciousness so it's most natural to just start at the beginning" ([Glitter, 2019](https://glittermagazine.co/2019/06/06/interview-tessa-violet-discusses-her-new-single-i-like-the-idea-of-you/)).
2. **Solo at piano/guitar** — "Mostly I write by myself on guitar or piano because I'm not good at top lining" (writing melody over an existing track); ~half of songs solo, half co-written with Lauren & Solomon Olds (Yacht Money) ([Vancouver Sun, 2023](https://vancouversun.com/entertainment/qa-singer-songwriter-tessa-violet-talks-my-god)). Lyrics and melody written together so words land "with the same cadence they'd be said" ([Starry, 2018](https://starrymag.com/tessa-violet-bad-ideas/)).
3. **Daily timed practice** — during Bad Ideas she "was in the habit of writing every day 9-2, just to get something out and practice the craft whether or not I liked it" ([Starry, 2018](https://starrymag.com/tessa-violet-bad-ideas/)).
4. **Idea/title first (later era)** — "For me, it starts with the idea. What am I trying to say?" ([Starry, 2025](https://starrymag.com/tessa-violet-my-bodys-my-buddy/)); the pre-write ritual: "what am I trying to say, who am I writing to, what do I wish I'd said to them? And how do I feel now?" ([15questions, 2023](https://15questions.net/interview/fifteen-questions-interview-tessa-violet/)).
5. **Discovery/channeling mindset** — "the song already exists, like a dinosaur skeleton under the dirt and my job is to brush all the dirt away" ([Starry, 2018](https://starrymag.com/tessa-violet-bad-ideas/)); "your job as a writer isn't to invent something from nothing, but to channel it" ([Sweety High, 2026](https://www.sweetyhigh.com/read/tessa-violet-sing-big-meaning-interview-080626); [Harvard Crimson, 2023](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2023/7/11/tessa-violet-journey-from-youtube-to-music-profile/)).
6. **Non-judgmental drafting** — "singing the wrong thing (the wrong lyric, the wrong melody), that's just part of it… the point of a first draft isn't to 'be good', it's just to exist" ([Sweety High, 2026](https://www.sweetyhigh.com/read/tessa-violet-sing-big-meaning-interview-080626)). A 5-day co-writing retreat that yielded zero songs — she was quietly previewing ideas instead of singing them — became the lesson of "Sing Big": "You gotta be willing to sing big if you want the ideas to come!" (Sweety High; her vlog [it's not that I'm afraid to fail...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mR3AjJj3oH0), 2026-08-04, auto-captions).
7. **Listener A/B tests** — "Sometimes I'll have a choice and I like both options, so I will send it to 7 people and see what hits people better" ([15questions, 2023](https://15questions.net/interview/fifteen-questions-interview-tessa-violet/)).
8. **Producer handoff** — finished songs go to long-time producer Seth Earnest ([Starry, 2018](https://starrymag.com/tessa-violet-bad-ideas/)); 2026 album *There Will Be Joy* produced by Cavetown and Brye, released one song per month with companion vlogs and national-park live videos ([Sweety High, 2026](https://www.sweetyhigh.com/read/tessa-violet-sing-big-meaning-interview-080626); [EARMILK, 2026](https://earmilk.com/2026/01/12/tessa-violet-teases-next-album-with-knowing-new-single/)).

## 3. Real Song Examples
- **Crush** — opening line from a real moment: "I'd been sitting at my piano for two hours scrolling through this guy's Instagram… 'I really cannot focus on what needs to get done'" ([Starry, 2018](https://starrymag.com/tessa-violet-bad-ideas/); [Coup de Main, 2019](https://www.coupdemainmagazine.com/tessa-violet/15439)); 113M views. Co-writers Solomon & Lauren Olds supplied the "permanent vacation" line ([Coup de Main, 2019](https://www.coupdemainmagazine.com/tessa-violet/15439)).
- **Bad Ideas** — first line "I hope you don't think I'm rude, but I wanna make out with you" paraphrased from something she'd actually said; written mostly in one night and "came together in a day" with Seth ([Coup de Main, 2019](https://www.coupdemainmagazine.com/tessa-violet/15439); [Starry, 2018](https://starrymag.com/tessa-violet-bad-ideas/)).
- **Games** — from a cheating breakup: "about the tension of suspecting something is up but being paralyzed because it doesn't feel good to call someone a liar" ([Atwood, 2021](https://atwoodmagazine.com/tsvg-tessa-violet-games-interview-music-2021/)).
- **Kitchen Song** — voice-memo version released as official audio; "a kitchen song is something you wrote on your own… for the joy of writing… versus thinking about 'How will people receive this?'" ([Daily Illini, 2023](https://dailyillini.com/buzz-stories/music/2023/08/29/tessa-violet-tour-album/)).
- **My Body's My Buddy** — title + concept first, "came together super fast" with Brye ([Starry, 2025](https://starrymag.com/tessa-violet-my-bodys-my-buddy/)).
- **Sing Big** — meta-process song about the retreat lesson (Sweety High, 2026).

## 5. Proven vs Claimed
Proven: the same first-line-first method is documented at both ends of her career and produced Crush (113M views; "200M+ aggregate streams" per [Starry 2025](https://starrymag.com/tessa-violet-my-bodys-my-buddy/)) plus three in-window releases ≥100k (above) — repeat 100k+ outcomes across a decade. Claimed, not proven: channeling/"song already exists" is unverifiable philosophy; the "sing big" lesson rests on a single retreat event; she states "I haven't had a success like that since the release of 'Crush'" ([Starry 2025](https://starrymag.com/tessa-violet-my-bodys-my-buddy/)) — 100M-scale repeatability is absent; no canonical framework published (no Song Exploder episode; gap).
