## 2. Step-by-Step Workflow

Anchored in **And The Writer Is… Ep. 226** (Ross Golan, 2025-10-27; [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yzWbab28z38), auto-captions) + MusicRadar:

1. **Concept-frame first (album level)** — LA exhibition "All About Love" (in response to bell hooks's book she "devoured") → "an album in response to the book and the exhibition… a case study almost on love… I could document the last two years of my life" (ATWI ~59:00).
2. **Residency writing** — "rent a house for eight weeks" with trusted collaborators ("House of Loving" — [Songfacts](https://www.songfacts.com/facts/olivia-dean/so-easy-to-fall-in-love)); Man I Need came from a one-week house session with Zach Nahome + Tobias Jesso Jr (MusicRadar, 2025-11-03).
3. **Intent-setting** — "I want to make something I can dance to… when I perform live it's just gonna be like a Tangfastic" + a reference: MJ's "The Way You Make Me Feel" (MusicRadar; ATWI ~1:10:00).
4. **Seed from the room** — sat at the Wurly, played the opening chords, "then we kind of just, like, wrote it"; the hook was written on camera, back-and-forth with Jesso Jr "punctuating the rhythm" (MusicRadar; ATWI ~1:11:10).
5. **Repeat-listening quality test** — "driving to and from the studio… I just played it again and again… if I can listen to this again and again I think other people might want to" (MusicRadar).
6. **Lyric rules** — reality barometer: "if I wouldn't say it out loud, then I probably wouldn't use it as a lyric… I'm not a great abstract person" ([Dork via MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/artists/a-barometer-is-if-i-wouldnt-say-it-out-loud-then-i-probably-wouldnt-use-it-as-a-lyric-how-olivia-deans-joyous-breakthrough-song-was-crafted)); scare-test: "The lyrics that you're scared to say are probably the best ones" ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2025/09/29/nx-s1-5464731/olivia-dean-the-art-of-loving)); "songwriting is really about just reading people" — for material and inside the room (ATWI ~55:40).
7. **Collection instinct** — self-described "songwriting sieve": "my brain's a sieve and the world's going in. I'm the sieve that's like: 'That's a song and that's a lyric'" ([NME, 2021](https://www.nme.com/blogs/nme-radar/olivia-dean-interview-2986658)); songs from overheard conversations, "the small things" (BBC Newsbeat).

## 3. Real Song Examples

- **Man I Need** — full origin story above (drum beat → Wurly chords → written same day); she initially doubted it ("it felt different… uptempo and sexy" — [MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/artists/i-think-it-was-a-song-that-i-wasnt-sure-about-at-first-it-felt-different-you-know-uptempo-and-sexy-but-i-love-it-olivia-dean-admits-that-she-initially-had-her-doubts-about-her-current-global-hit-single)).
- **Ladies Room** — overheard in a pub toilet; she voice-recorded the stranger (later illegal to use); favourite lyric "If you can't see my mirrors, then I can't see you" from truck stickers ([BBC Newsbeat](https://www.bbc.com/news/newsbeat-67495800), [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/london/music/olivia-dean-interview-2023)).
- **Dive** — deliberate mood-flip: "I want to write something light" after "a phase of writing a lot of quite sad breakup music"; Motown/Diana Ross reference (Genius via MusicRadar).
- **The Hardest Part** — pandemic Zoom co-write; "I hated that song… this is cringe"; manager: "It's really good" — a self-doubt loop she says "happens a lot" (ATWI ~39:15).
- **Loud** — scariest lyric: "single vocal, one take, front to back," never sung again (NPR).
- **So Easy (To Fall In Love)** — "a pat on the back when you're going out the door on your first date," from watching friends fret over being liked ([Elle](https://www.elle.com/culture/music/a68068522/olivia-dean-interview/)).
- **Nice To Each Other** — "there's no reason why the word *cutlery* should be in Nice To Each Other… but I knew that Matt [Hales] would understand that" — private-reality lyrics (ATWI).

## 4. Tools & Templates

Voice-memo field recording (Ladies Room); concept-frame template (album as response/case study to a book); residency template (rented house, 8 weeks) with rotating co-writers (Nahome, Jesso Jr, Max Wolfgang — 7 songs on the album; Amy Allen/John Ryan on So Easy — [Songfacts](https://www.songfacts.com/facts/olivia-dean/so-easy-to-fall-in-love)); piano-tight test ("if the song is good… you can play it just on the piano" — [Sound of Boston interview, via blog recap](https://www.musicmusingsandsuch.com/musicmusingsandsuch/2023/11/28/feature-over-the-hardest-part-reflecting-on-a-successful-and-important-year-for-the-remarkable-olivia-dean)); surfing metaphor — "you can train yourself to get on the board. If the waves aren't there, you're not going to surf today" (ibid.).

## 5. Proven vs Claimed

Proven: the documented method (intent + reference track + room-energy co-write + repeat-listen test) produced Man I Need — UK #1, Hot 100 #2, 107M YouTube views; the bell hooks frame + House of Loving produced a platinum-UK/gold-US album ([ELLE SG](https://elle.com.sg/life-culture/entertainment/olivia-dean-on-her-newfound-fame-writing-feminist-romance/)) with three simultaneous UK Top-10s — "first British woman since Adele" (NPR); the self-doubt→outside-validation loop repeated (Hardest Part, Man I Need). Claimed, not proven: "scared lyrics are best" and the sieve metaphor are self-reported; no source shows the method working beyond her own catalogue.
