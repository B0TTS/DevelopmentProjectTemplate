## 2. Step-by-Step Workflow

Synthesized from four primary sources (Song Exploder ep. 291; Billboard "How It Went Down"; Variety Hitmakers 2022; Music Week 2023):

1. **Start from the thesis, not the beat.** "What song does the world need to hear and that I need to sing?… How do I honor that sound?" (SE transcript). For *Special* the brief was "a declaration… give everybody a warm hug" after lockdown (Variety).
2. **Vent-first, filter-second.** She writes dark "get it off your chest" songs the world "don't need to hear," then the hopeful record that matters: "the songwriter kicks in and you're like, how do I make this for all of us?" (SE).
3. **Open with the real event.** "Truth Hurts" started from a breakup voicemail she recounted to Ricky Reed, who told her, "I hope you know you just wrote a song" — he scribbled her venting into lyrics (Billboard). "I can't write songs that don't resonate with me on a personal level" (SE).
4. **Start with a lyric + a feeling, no music.** "Still Bad" began as a country joke ("I don't need him, I need a drink," written by Sam Harris) that Lizzo reframed as "a breakup song with the world" (SE).
5. **Write every lyric possible, then whittle.** "We have to write every lyric possible… and then we're like, okay, let's whittle it down" (SE).
6. **"Lizzo-isms" gate.** "A song ain't finished until we get them" — her signature one-liners only she would say, e.g. the "Balenci-ussy's" second-verse line Theron Thomas first misheard; offbeat/unusual lines are treated as viral fuel (Music Week).
7. **Multi-version demo churn.** "Still Bad" went through 5+ complete re-skins (country → Gnarls-Barkley synth → piano → bassline → rock): "we would work on one for a week, two weeks, and then be like, this isn't really getting there. Let's try a different version" (SE). "About Damn Time": lyric+melody alone took "a couple of months of day-in, day-out," and a bridge she worked on 5 full days got cut (Variety). "Months and months of making every melody and every lyric exactly what she wanted to say" (MWTM teaser, 5:04).
8. **Vowel-level melody-adjacent edits.** Hook lead-in changed from "Think I'm acting different? / Watch me double down" to "Feeling crazy lately, baby, let's go out" — "the vowel sound is just so much more fun to sing… it's so tedious, but I always feel like it yields the best results" (SE).
9. **Record dry.** Before singing she demanded "no tune, no reverb, no delay — dry," calling effects "training wheels"; she layeringed multiple "characters" (bratty, nasally, airy, full-bodied) to fake a party gang (MWTM teaser; SE).
10. **Final filter: message + body-feel.** "The negativity in this is not something I think the world needs right now. It's off" (Variety); Reed's test: "it has to go through the body… does it make me want to move?" (Variety).

## 3. Real Song Examples

- **"Truth Hurts"** — voicemail-to-song; sung "like playing tag with herself," running into the booth to drop lines as she thought of them; title came from listening back — "Truth hurts, boy. Truth hurts" (Billboard How It Went Down video series).
- **"About Damn Time"** — full step-by-step: E-flat-minor-9 chord omitting the third so the song "doesn't spell out happy or sad," bass groove, then Lizzo's lyric/melody pass (Variety; MWTM teaser).
- **"Still Bad"** — the 5-demo evolution, Franz-Ferdinand turn, "Plot twist, I'm doing great" as her favorite "cheeky" line, gang-vocal/harmony experiments (Song Exploder ep. 291, transcript PDF).
- **"Love in Real Life"** — the turning-point record that set the album's sound: first "things are gonna be all right" song after months (SE).

## 4. Tools & Templates

- **Ricky Reed's "living room setup" co-write circle** — no booth divide, instruments in a circle, "kumbaya" (Variety).
- **The gang-vocal tradition** — calling friends in to yell ("Yaya-ee! Woo!"), and the multi-character vocal-layer cheat when no friends are available (SE).
- **The "dry vocal" rule** — no pitch correction/reverb on the scratch, performance-first (MWTM teaser).
- **Remote co-write loop** — beat sent to co-writer (Theron Thomas in Atlanta), ideas exchanged, finished in LA (Music Week).
- No named templates/software published. Paid course exists — **Mix With The Masters "Writing a hit song with Lizzo"** (Ricky Reed + Blake Slatkin, Westlake Studio D) — existence only; free 3-min teaser is public (captions saved).

## 5. Proven vs Claimed

**Proven (repeat, measured):** 17 own-channel releases ≥100k in 24 months; "Truth Hurts" 7-week Hot 100 #1; "About Damn Time" Grammy Record of the Year 2023; same method documented across three eras (2017 → 2022 → 2025-26) by three independent co-writer accounts (Reed, Slatkin, Thomas).

**Claimed (asserted, not audited):** the vent-first/world-needs-filter (impossible to measure); "Lizzo-isms as a finish-gate" is Thomas's account of house culture, not a verified rule; the E-minor-9 "message vehicle" theory is Reed's retrospective reading. **Gaps:** no Genius Verified episodes exist for Lizzo (none found on Genius); no bar-by-bar rhyme/flow breakdowns published; pool lead "Song Exploder ep. 291 = Truth Hurts" is WRONG — it's "Still Bad" (the Truth Hurts story comes from Billboard instead); Song Exploder covers one song; the LIRL album-track official audios (Sep 2025) mostly sit below 100k — her cadence is album-cycle, not singles-mill.
