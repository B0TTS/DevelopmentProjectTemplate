## 2. Step-by-Step Workflow

AJR's documented lyric craft is a two-person loop — Ryan starts, Jack filters:

1. **Life becomes source material.** A real annoyed sentence Ryan blurted at Jack in a college library ("would you put your phone down") became "Way Less Sad"'s trumpet melody; home-movie voices were sampled into it — "it took 20 years to create this song" (Making of Way Less Sad, 00:02:49–00:04:40). A door knock became "Bang!"'s snare/kick (Making of Bang!, 00:00:19–00:00:48).
2. **Ryan makes the "raw clay."** "More often than not I'm starting the songs like I'm making the raw clay… the first version I show Jack" (How I Write, 00:14:41); he is "the sort of emotional lyric writer… between us" (00:01:41).
3. **Voice-memo melody auditions.** "As Jack often does, he sent me a voice memo" — two attempts rejected ("that wasn't catchy at all… less catchy than the first one"); catchiness is the gate (Making of Way Less Sad, 00:03:56–00:04:23).
4. **Universal-vignette lyric theory.** "Little vignettes that you just know it when you hear it… the right 10 words in a row and the right melody, you're just like, I believe you" (How I Write, 00:12:34–00:12:45).
5. **Character-first for emotional songs.** "With a dog song, I have to start with, okay, there's a character here that thinks he's protecting us. We're actually protecting him. What's this story?" — the *A Dog Song* / *World's Smallest Violin* method (How I Write, 00:25:24–00:25:45).
6. **Jack's attention filter on lyrics.** Ryan's original *World's Smallest Violin* line ("that's the way it always is and that's the way it's always been") was cut because Jack "felt lectured at… it feels very Broadway"; the conversational "Oh my god, that's so insane…" replaced it (How I Write, 00:15:00–00:15:28).
7. **Anti-formula structure.** "We're sort of bored by verse, chorus, verse, chorus, bridge, chorus… none of the songs on this new EP adhere to that structure… you've already heard this chorus two times. Let's do a crazy outro" (How I Write, 00:34:52–00:35:26).
8. **Volume + themed constraints.** "Write a lot of bad songs is the idea" (01:07:57); *The Click* = "write a lot of songs about things that have never been written about before" (American Songwriter); *The Maybe Man*'s concept was "found along the way" (01:29:25).
9. **Everything in the living room.** "We make everything in our living room" (Making of Bang!, 00:00:02); the shared apartment is where "we actually write all of the songs" (American Songwriter), with daily write-and-produce sessions during COVID isolation.

## 3. Real Song Examples

- **"World's Smallest Violin"** — "probably our biggest hit" (How I Write, 00:14:45; 170M+ MV views per episode intro); dog-perspective character song; the documented lyric-rewrite above.
- **"Bang!"** — began as "a very simple piano line," then a "modern snap" replaced by "big 808s and big snares"; hired NYC subway announcer Charlie Pellett for the "here we go"/"metronome" chorus announcement (Ryan's TikTok, via ABC Audio); concept = "not quite a kid… not quite an adult yet" (American Songwriter). 2x-platinum, Billboard Top Rock Song.
- **"Way Less Sad"** — the phone-down phrase → trumpet melody → voice-memo catchiness filter chain, with home-movie audio as the finishing layer.
- **"The Dumb Song"** — 18-month process filmed in their studio (Oct 2021 → final mix approved in their car), the full write→produce→mix arc shown in the MV itself (Wikipedia).
- **"A Dog Song" / "Betty" / "The Big Goodbye"** — 2025 EP tracks built on the character-first method and structure rule (How I Write).

## 4. Tools & Templates

- **Living room / shared apartment** as permanent studio (all albums).
- **Voice memos** as the melody-capture and peer-review medium (Jack → Ryan, catchiness filter).
- **Found sounds** (door knock, home-movie audio, real spoken phrases) as hooks and samples.
- **Emulation→fusion apprenticeship**: "hundreds, if not thousands of songs" copying Simon & Garfunkel, Kendrick Lamar, Beach Boys, then fusing "this from Kendrick, but this from The Beach Boys" (Line of Best Fit).
- **Album concept goals** as lyric constraint lists (*The Click* rule); self-release label AJR Productions for the 2025 EP.

## 5. Proven vs Claimed

**Proven (repeat, measured):** 4 in-window own-channel releases ≥100k; *World's Smallest Violin* 170M+ views; "Bang!" 2x-platinum + Billboard Top Rock Song; 5B+ streams (Line of Best Fit; episode intro); the same method spans four albums + an EP (2015→2025), including the in-window 2025 singles.

**Claimed (self-asserted):** the "right 10 words in a row" rule and the voice-memo catchiness gate are aesthetic heuristics with no published failure data; "write a lot of bad songs" is self-reported; no published syllable/rhyme methodology. **Gaps:** Zach Sang's *The Maybe Man* breakdown (1:15:56) has no captions — not quotable; the official "Making of Bang!" video is production-side only (no lyric content); *The Dumb Song* MV captions are lyrics only — its timeline is reconstructed via Wikipedia/ABC Audio.
