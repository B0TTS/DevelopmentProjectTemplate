## 2. Step-by-Step Workflow

As documented by Puth himself (four primary sources):

1. **Harvest everyday speech.** "The general approach of how I think about writing is just to say something that people already say… people might inadvertently say something really dope and it might turn into a cool lyric" — his "song assistant method": words listeners need to say (Studio "HIT Chorus" video, 0:00–1:20).
2. **Chorus first, always.** "We'll start with the chorus first and then build around that. That's what I usually do"; his DAW template exists "because I always start with the chorus" (same video, 1:40).
3. **Syllables before meaning.** "I have a very Swedish approach to lyrics, so sometimes my initial scratched lyrics make no sense. As long as the syllables sound good" (Genius Verified "Attention", 2017). Confirmed live 2025: "it also sounds good off the tongue… I don't know what it means yet."
4. **Babble, lock rhythm, words later.** He sings placeholder babble to test pockets, then trims syllables on the grid: "You want it to be as easy to sing along to as possible. That's why I'm okay with babble for now."
5. **Sync words with melody.** "If I sing up and the melody goes up, there's some innate catchiness attached to that."
6. **Vary rhyme scheme per chorus half** ("should sound like a roller coaster… A good chorus should literally just mirror itself"); delete repeated words ("too many goes").
7. **Write the truth.** "Everything that you put into your art should be 100% real… I've written songs that maybe weren't the truth. And those didn't resonate at all with people" (HIT Chorus). Reversed his early approach: 2017 "I kind of had to Taylor Swift it" → 2026 "the more general you are, the less people relate" (Vogue).
8. **Title first.** "I think it's title first… I have a conversation with myself… I have to tell the truth." Titles incubate: "Don't Meet Your Heroes" "I remember having that title in my head for a very long time" (Vogue, 2026).
9. **Patience + showing up.** "It's okay if a week goes by and nothing happens… We have to search constantly for inspiration. We have to show up for it." "My process varies… One day it'll take a month. It just patience is key" (NAMM 2026 fan Q&A).

## 3. Real Song Examples

- **"See You Again"** — written after friend Vale's death: "we're just trading lyrics — it's been a long day without you my friend… then we have a completed chorus," then 8 months of trying other singers before keeping his own vocal (NAMM Q&A; EW/LA Times 2015; Wikipedia).
- **"Attention"** — from a real hotel-room scene in the Philippines (a girl crying about her ex); the title line "She just wants attention" is the scene's verdict (Genius Verified).
- **"We Don't Talk Anymore"** — the fireplace co-write: "I didn't write that chorus… I have no ego about it" — and its raw demo vocal "ended up being in the actual song" (HIT Chorus).
- **"Changes"** — full 42-min NAMM/Mix With The Masters breakdown; about a changing friendship (MWTM; Vogue).
- **"I Used To Be Cringe" / "Don't Meet Your Heroes"** — title-first specificity era; the former written after Taylor Swift's "Charlie Puth should be a bigger artist" lyric (Vogue).
- **Live course chorus** — "Why you got to go and get my hopes up": the whole method executed from zero on camera (Studio, 2025).

**Proven (repeat, measured):** 154/154 own-channel uploads ≥100k views (max 3.5B); every 2025–26 release cleared ≥100k; 9 multiplatinum singles, 35B+ streams (MWTM intro); "STAY" (producer credit) longest-reigning Billboard #1 (studio.com page). Method sustains output across eras (2015→2026).
