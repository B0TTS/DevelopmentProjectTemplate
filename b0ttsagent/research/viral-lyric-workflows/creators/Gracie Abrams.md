## 2. Step-by-Step Workflow

Documented across Song Exploder ep. 283 (transcript PDF, anchor source), her own "Story of My Song" series, and interviews:

1. **Capture anywhere**: ideas go straight to the iPhone **Notes app** (lyrics) or **Voice Memos** app (melody) as they strike — "I just look like a crazy person all the time writing ideas in my Notes app or leaving the room for ten seconds to yell into my phone" ([Coup de Main, 2020](https://www.coupdemainmagazine.com/gracie-abrams/16280)); "The voice memo app for sure. Or the notes app... interchangeable" ([BuzzFeed 30 Questions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xRGZa9oyXfM), via auto-captions).
2. **Germination**: a first line arrives with chords spontaneously; she grabs the guitar mid-conversation ([Song Exploder transcript](https://songexploder.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Song-Exploder-Gracie-Abrams-Transcript.pdf), "I told Audrey, 'hold on,' ran inside, grabbed the guitar"). Acoustic guitar is her default writing instrument — "the most portable" ([BuzzFeed 30 Questions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xRGZa9oyXfM)).
3. **Co-write with Audrey Hobert** (friend since age 10, roommate): blunt, fast exchange; "we're oftentimes going with the line that makes us laugh the hardest"; the co-writer's "fuck yes, let's chase it" energy validates half-formed ideas ([Song Exploder transcript](https://songexploder.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Song-Exploder-Gracie-Abrams-Transcript.pdf)).
4. **Voice memo as working document**: she mumble-sings between speaking and singing, testing "mouth-feel" of words; full-song demos are recorded (both singing) ([Song Exploder transcript](https://songexploder.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Song-Exploder-Gracie-Abrams-Transcript.pdf)). Lyrics get written "really, really quick... you're kind of just pulling it out of thin air a little bit and then there's a mini blackout period" ([Story of My Song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ugT-APo4ow), auto-captions).
5. **Structure devices she names**: songs as "chapters" (past/present/future verses); "manic" bridges where the "pendulum... every 5 seconds" swings; double hooks planned in advance; callbacks to earlier sections "tweaking it to keep the narrative moving forward" ([Story of My Song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ugT-APo4ow)).
6. **Material**: vivid personal specifics (blue eyes, a recent haircut) as lyric fodder; journal entries as source; she checks in with the people songs are about ([Story of My Song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ugT-APo4ow); [The Independent, 2022](https://www.independent.co.uk/arts-entertainment/music/features/gracie-abrams-interview-phoebe-bridgers-b2108609.html)).
7. **Production handoff**: Aaron Dessner at Long Pond Studio; a raw memo can become a song whose original melody is gone entirely ([Billboard "Voice Memo Demo", 2026](https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/gracie-abrams-interview-daughter-from-hell-mews-sober-1236296116/)). Live shows double as lyric testing — "I did that once or twice" got its punchy "or twice" from crowd reaction at The Greek ([Story of My Song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ugT-APo4ow)).

## 3. Real Song Examples

- **"I Love You, I'm Sorry"** — broken down on [Song Exploder 283](https://songexploder.net/gracie-abrams): born from "Two Augusts ago" + chords; verse "chapters" ("Two Augusts ago" → "Two summers from now"); bridge as "manic thought perspective"; title follows her earlier "I Miss You, I'm Sorry" — "a pillar" she iterated as "I Hate You, I'm Sorry" versions for years.
- **"That's So True"** — [Story of My Song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ugT-APo4ow): notes-app screenshot shows lyrics written fast; hook "that's so true" lifted from her and Audrey's own speaking habit; double-chorus placement.
- **"Sober" / "Mews"** — [Billboard Voice Memo Demo](https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/gracie-abrams-interview-daughter-from-hell-mews-sober-1236296116/): a cappella hummed memos sparked by Dessner instrumentals; "Sober" kept no trace of its original melody.

## 4. Tools & Templates

- **iPhone Notes app** (lyrics/titles) and **Voice Memos** (melodies, mumble demos) — the two named tools ([Coup de Main](https://www.coupdemainmagazine.com/gracie-abrams/16280), [BuzzFeed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xRGZa9oyXfM))
- **Journal** — "an archive of feelings"; she advises 10 minutes a day ([Dazed, 2023](https://www.dazeddigital.com/music/article/58280/1/gracie-abrams-good-riddance-album); [Teen Vogue, 2023](https://www.teenvogue.com/story/gracie-abrams-good-riddance-eras-tour-interview))
- **Title-as-pillar template**: same title format iterated across songs (I Miss You / I Love You, I'm Sorry)
- **Co-writer riff format** with a close friend (Hobert on The Secret of Us; Dessner on all three albums; Mescal co-credit on "Imaginary Friend" — [Rolling Stone, 2026](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-features/gracie-abrams-daughter-from-hell-upbringing-relationship-1235600799/))

## 5. Proven vs Claimed

Proven: repeat success at scale — three albums (Good Riddance 2023, The Secret of Us 2024, Daughter From Hell 2026, Billboard No. 1 per [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-features/gracie-abrams-daughter-from-hell-upbringing-relationship-1235600799/)) and 10+ in-window uploads ≥100k. The workflow's persistence is documented across eras: voice-memo capture (2020 Coup de Main) through raw memos aired on Song Exploder (2024) and Billboard (2026). Claimed, not proven: her "pulling it out of thin air" description is self-reported, and no source demonstrates the method working for anyone other than herself. The framework is heavily relationship-dependent (Hobert co-writing), so its transferability is unproven.
