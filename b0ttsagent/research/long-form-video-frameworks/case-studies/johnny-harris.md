## 1. The 4-month pipeline (claimed) — what he actually says it is

> "it's about a four month process for each video" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM)

His walkthrough, step-for-step in order uttered [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM):

1. **Story day** — "roll our sleeves up and ... look at story pitches and ideas that we've been taking notes on for a long time"
2. **Greenlight + reporting brief in Slack** — "I Greenlight it on a channel in slack I say it's greenlit and often I deliver a reporting brief" — example last night on airplane "El Chapo's prison escape ... I want to know everything about this tunnels" after "a tiny Google to be like what's interesting and then ask a million questions"
3. **Researcher 3-4 weeks → info doc** — "researcher spends 3 to four weeks becoming an expert ... surfacing visuals building out what we call an info doc which is like a 60 to 80 page digest"
4. **He wrestles / curates / outlines** — "I process it in my brain I wrestle with it and I start to curate and outline and think about it as a story" — sometimes additional research / expert interview or two with him
5. **Scripting week — 3-4 dedicated days hardcore writing** — "it is me in a room completely engrossed ... writing and Visually directing and art directing" — "after those 3 to four days there is a lot of visual assets that need to be gathered there's a lot of factchecking ... sometimes additional reporting"
6. **Production kickoff → filming → post** — "there's a production kickoff where I translate the vision to a leader in our post-production team they grab that vision and they carry it through I film it the footage goes off and then they start to build out everything that was put on that piece of paper"
7. **Cuts: shame cut → rough → fine → fine two → audio lock → picture lock** — "it starts with a shame cut which is just a radio edit an assembly with some very crude visuals and then it goes into a rough cut ... into a fine cut ... into a fine two ... into an audio lock a picture lock and each of those we are leaving 150 notes or whatever ... Google Docs frame Frame IO"
8. **Thumbnail + sponsorship slotted** — "thumbnail starts to get developed within that at some point and the sponsorship we do an ad read and that gets slotted in"
9. **Scheduling** — "that's a well oiled machine that just works and it's all slotted in and plotted you know 6 months in advance and then the video goes live ... and then you do that 30 times a year"

**Verified vs claimed / thin:** 4 months, 60-80 pages, 3-4 weeks researcher time, 150 notes per cut, 6 months plotted, 30/year — all self-reported in one take, not audited. Evidence JSON shows median publish interval ~3-4 weeks in 2026 (2026-08-13, 2026-07-30, 2026-07-16, 2026-06-18...), consistent with ~30/year claim but not proving 4-month overlap depth. No doc tracks throughput (WIP limit) numerically — thin.

## 3. Idea capture — story days, notes, and the pendulum on field preparation

**Story day** is the gate: review pitches and long-running notes, then greenlight + reporting brief [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM) (see §1).

**Notes from the field** are voice-first: "I'm talking to my phone for minutes ... I'm talking to transcription and ... realize that like it didn't ... press the microphone" — Apple Notes transcription, reflecting after every interview where "every interview I have changes my mind and so it's this Whiplash" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM). When writing/researching at computer, less whiplash; when in field talking to humans "everything is chaos and to into something meaningful which always leaves so much complexity on the ground" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).

**Preparation pendulum** — he narrates a 10-year arc [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM):

- Early field: "show up and let the story come to you ... I would do a lot of story work in the edit"
- Vox Borders professionalization: "I would prepro the hell out of every story ... ambitious shot ... drone shot ... need to get a sound bite ... had a lot of benefits ... I got way better at getting crazy cool shots but I started to over engineer"
- "three or four years into that I felt like I was over engineering and shoehorning the story into my scaffold"
- Now swinging back: Finland in a couple weeks "I'm intentionally just not not like deciding what I want to say or even a framing ... I'm going to train with the Finnish Army ... Let It Flow and then I'm going to decide what I want to say"
- Contrast with Saudi Arabia: "did read a lot ... couple books ... couple experts ... but I didn't do any storytelling work ... knowledge is one thing storytelling is the curation of chaotic information ... into a very firm linear presentation"

**What is NOT documented:** no scoring rubric for greenlight beyond "what we've been taking notes on for a long time" — thin, intuitive.

## 4. Packaging is a promise — defined early, tested, but not the story

> "the marketing which is what this is this is why should you click is defined early on" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM)

He frames the thumbnail/title as a billboard, not the story:

> "I knew that what would bring them in is why is Saudi Arabia building a futuristic city in the desert ... that I knew that that was the promise that was a clear promise the storytelling is a complicated thing this is not a complicated thing this is like a this is the Billboard this is the packaging" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM)

Process details [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM):

- "I usually will do two or three different packagings when I'm deciding what story to do and be like does this have legs for to be clickable but that doesn't Define the story it just defines kind of one thing that I know I'm going to answer"
- "**Market vs passion**" — "a video has to be marketable ... a couple million people have to be interested" — he does "seven titles or more" for the Mormon video, almost all starting "how or why because we're in the business of answering questions and explaining processes" — wording is "very important" and "it's a promise"
- **Tools / comps:** "sometimes we'll check comps on one of 10 which is like a ... shows videos in this topic that have done well and how they've been packaged" — "Mormons ... this video about Mormons did 50x the normal views ... because ... it was packaged like this" — "I think view stats is another"
- **AB testing now vs before:** "now that there's AB testing we want to have very different directions so we can test did the people want the map or did they want the Illuminati whereas before we were always tinkering with just one ... and like refining it" — thumb thoughts include imagery directions: "Illuminati energy ... map"

**Payoff theory** — two answers, one controversial [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM):

- Controversial: "if I can get someone to click that I can ... convince them to brought in their curiosity to a billion other things ... resource curse and roner states and ... history of oil ... you will have forgotten that that's why you clicked ... because you will be let into this much bigger world ... I'm folding in the vegetables"
- Straightforward: "I still do need to satisfyingly answer the question for those who don't have that first experience ... I don't answer it in the first 10 seconds or the first minute but by the end ... I have to ... I have to reinforce the promise of the thumbnail within the first minute I would say"

And the channel-level promise: "my favorite comment is I had no idea I would watch a 20 minute video about ice cream machines" — "if you can find the Beauty and the enthusiasm in ... mundane things life becomes a lot more interesting" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).

**Caveat:** No numeric packaging test doc — one-of-10 / ViewStats mentioned as glance tools, not as SOP with thresholds. Exact click target ("couple million") is illustrative, not reported hit-rate — thin.

## 5. Open / hook — drop into action, emotion, and "look at this"

He rejects textbook cold-open formulas:

> "there's never I've never identified with these kind of classical formats ... of like rising action and ... climax ... I've never identified with those ... I don't actually believe in some ultimate format I think that's ... a myth" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM)

What he does do [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM):

- **First line = action:** "I like first lines that drop you into action that enliven the viewer's ... Senses with a surprising usually a surprising action or Visual and often times that starts with look at this ... it immediately engages the viewer to say ... do something" — Mormon example: "it started with the murder of their prophet in Illinois" — "a line that wakes you up ... I'm not saying like I want to tell you the story ... I don't want to do like a classical ... tell them what you're going to tell them you start with action" — on paper he has "gunshot epic suspensful yes slow-mo I wanted this to be emotional ... you can drop someone in immediately to an emotional tone"
- **Inviting frame:** "I want to show you what this map says and show you how China uses craphy to assert and project power" does "equalizing the journalist and the viewer I want to show you I'm a friend who's really curious" vs detached adversarial journalist distance [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM)
- **Hook device without saying it:** "there's a lot more going on with this map I'm going to show it to you that ... that's a promise ... absolutely one of the themes of your writing is you are constantly saying there's a lot more going on ... without ever saying that explicitly ... that's a kind of a retention thing ... foreshadowing" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM)

**Thin:** No timestamped hook template (e.g., 0:00-0:30 must do X), no retention graph cited — see §11.

## 6. Structure — history backbone when you have it, wrestling when you don't

> "This is a history video I not all my videos are history videos this one the writing has a backbone inherently which takes away ... what is going to be the ... order ... when it's ... chronological ... I know where I start and I know where I end the puzzle turns into what am I curating and how am I explaining it in a new way ... maybe a third of my videos are kind of history videos that have a really clean chronology and that's whenever it's a history video I'm like ah I don't have to wrestle with a structure thank God ... fighting structure is brutal" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM)

Before writing visuals, he curates an **outline** separate from visuals: "I've read the info doc I know what all the information is I'm going to now curate the sections that I think are going to go in I'm not thinking about visuals I'm just thinking about backbone and chronology and flow and promise" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).

Beyond history, weight: "It's often more plain and simple but it hits ... visual brain ... Pinker's ... sense of style ... is my entire Bible of writing so who did what to whom" — instead of "globalization ... we now send Coca-Cola to Thailand ... America's ... Ford to Japan ... an agent doing a thing that we can see" — that's the throughline for structure: agents acting, moving in space/time [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).

On maps, he makes the stage literal: "I think in terms of characters and ... on the map you will see that I build two icons ... one is the Mormons and ... one is the federal government ... They're far away ... but they're now both agents ... I call this loading up the icon with motives ... tell the viewer this is their agenda" — "The Mormons ... federal government ... there's tension ... the big federal government slowly starts to move west and ... we know that the Mormons are scared" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM). Then reductionist dialogue icon: "hey we know you didn't like us settling in Missouri ... but we're out here in the west ... please let us have our own state" — "a conversational modern-day language interpretation of what was really a four-year process of lobbying" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).

Length: "sometimes it's 20 minutes and sometimes it's 40 minutes and that's what I love about ... YouTube is ... no one's forcing me into a format" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM) — interview elsewhere claims average 28 mins (vs Shortlist's 15-30 niche/format). He quantifies output as "we make 30 videos a year ... average ... 28 minutes ... so ... like 15 minutes ... a week ... every minute is ... premium animation and custom music ... we have an amazing team and we also are not precious we just go ... do a few rounds and then we move on" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).

**Contradiction:** No fixed act vs very fixed pipeline (story day → info doc → outline → scripting week → art ingredients → shame → ... → lock) — he denies classical story format but embraces a rigid production format.

## 7. The two voices that pace every video

He names them explicitly [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM):

- **Explainer / active / thinky:** "who did what to whom" (Steven Pinker classic style), "very clear who did what to whom ... they fled instead of ... leading to the migration" — "our brains can see that they can see people fleeing", concrete visual active language [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).
- **Contemplative / poetic / fey:** "usually towards the end where I want to ... zoom out and reflect" — music "call it a conclusion energy ... today the descendants ... live in Mormon communities along this Corridor" — "less who did what to whom ... more ... the postcript" — then personal: "My wife and I went to school right here in the heart of it in this beautiful Valley ... feeling close to the specific sugarcoated version ... I was taught ... beautiful Valley I was brainwashed ... life is complicated ... throwing ... a wrench in ... one-sided interpretation" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).

Switching is the retention mechanic: "switching between the two from like a cognitive or thinky tone to a fey tone ... with visuals and in language ... makes the presentation more engaging more compelling ... people remember it for longer they remember what they felt" — example "the human wall that protected the country ... and might have to do so again the people who live among the undetonated bombs and the abandoned bones" — he shows bones searcher, goes with bomb squad, wraps "bombs and Bones next to each other" for emotional tone [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).

Economist model for the explainer voice [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM): new Syrian leader piece — context/history then "the palace which is five times larger than the White House is Echoes with emptiness and there wasn't even staff to serve coffee" — "a human moment ... rigorous analysis ... that little detail ... visual concrete ... that I want to hear."

Philosophy grounding: "everything is interesting everything has a story it just needs to have that life breathed into it" — he treats enthusiasm as contemplative craftsmanship that imbues soul late: "there's this moment ... where something gets imbued with the sense of Soul and Spirit late in the process and it kind of comes alive" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).

## 8. Retention in his own terms — three legs, no dashboard

He names the triad offhand when asked about surprise [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM) (interviewer paraphrases; he confirms):

> "if you can get those three components the visual the surprise the naturally interesting it's almost like you have this ... Tailwind behind you"

**1. Naturally interesting** — picking paradoxes and market test (§4) but also belief you can enliven curiosity they didn't know they had (§4).

**2. Visual** — see §9 and §10: "look at this" + circle/zoom/shine to "direct the eye" because "we're visual creatures we have a lot of our brain dedicated to visual processing so when they can have cognitive information ... and ... visual experience dancing together ... it's a really pleasant experience" — "Vox ... really pioneered ... leaning into that style ... I'm really grateful ... that was my boot camp" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).

**3. Surprise** — "the writing is ... some of the most important for making something surprising because when you are talking about something you can ... be an llm and ... fill in the next word ... or ... be a human and ... say this thing in a completely different way" — example Antarctica: "how did we get this a map so detailed ... an impossibility made manifest by superhumans in the future" vs wonky "history of ... satellite mapping ... you could just jargon the hell out of that ... what if we put the lens of pure beauty and awe" — analogy "is a canal ... bottom ... path of lease resistance ... if you want to do surprising language you have to climb up the wall of the canal and ... be ... on the bank ... harder" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).

Other retention-adjacent tactics his interview names without numbers [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM):

- **"There's a lot more going on"** implicit foreshadowing (see §5) — "hey stick around I promise this is going to get interesting ... foreshadowing ... so that you are compelled to stick around"
- **Vocal audience empathy** — "I'm always empathizing with the viewer I'm thinking who's watching ... what is happening in their minds ... that is the most important sensibility" — evidence: wrestling to include "husband to 40 wives" line (rhythm "music" but risks turning off believing Mormons) decides to include because "most ... will find that fascinating" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).
- **Let me show you / inviting peer framing** vs gatekeeper smart-jargon — "most people overestimate how much context the audience has and underestimates their intelligence and you should do the opposite ... most people have no idea ... but they're actually really smart" (Cleo Abram line he endorses) — "little subtle cues like let me show you ... set the tone" and "lets defenses down ... embracing ... hey all of us to learning together ... creates ... better absorption" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).
- **Plain concrete visual language** — "visual plain clear concrete yes ... YouTube is the original show and tell platform ... very warm room for let me show you ... more human ... more relatable and conversational the more concrete ... the platform were rewarded" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).

**Thin / not documented:** No CTR, AVD, AVP targets, no chapter card timing, no retention graph discussion, no A/B win-rate — he explicitly avoids that dashboard: "I'm not saying let's go talk about ... retention and the YouTube game ... that's a revealing thing that you're Focus so much on craftsmanship" interviewer notes, and Harris confirms "we definitely play the game but craftsmanship is number one" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM). Sources are thin on numeric retention — stated explicitly, not padded.

## 9. Visual script = writing + coding (in his lexicon)

> "I am writing words and then I'm immediately going over to the right column and ... coordinating choreographing visuals that will play that will dance with the pros that that is the only way I know do I've never been a text writer I've always been someone who writes for motion" and "every sentence or two has a new row ... and it gets its own specific Direction" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM)

Tooling as described [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM):

- **Google Docs two-column table** — left prose, right feeling/visuals; "I'm pressing Tab and I'm going to the right and I am coding the visuals" — "it's not writing I'm ... pressing all these buttons to make different colors and different indentations and I am thinking about animation ... speaking to my future animator"
- **Color-code macros:** "when it is red like this it is a visual asset that needs to be collected ... I have a macro ... when I'm writing and I press alt a and it makes it bold it turns it red and it turns it into a checkbox and that happens all in 0.01 seconds ... I am coding ... without ever pressing a mouse and that allows me to ... edit in my mind"
- **Annotations:** screenshots of maps, archival photos, references, "me with screenshots annotating an old map ... I'm basically creating the whole thing as a piece of visual Direction ... I call it coding because it feels ... like coding"
- **Fact-check as color:** "comments are our fact check ... every assertion ... has a fact check ... Everything is green ... every assertion ... was red at first ... but the moment it got fact checked it got a citation tag and if you click ... you see a little blurb ... and sourcing ... some ... have three or four fact checks from academic articles from Census Data ... this is the way we keep our facts really watertight ... we have a water type process now ... and we ... published these as a source dock that people can go look and scrutinize" — Mormon script example shows more comment words than prose [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM)

**Draft latency:** shame cut is "all the voice ... with ... screenshots of the right column happening in time so ... I can ... watch it ... and ... give feedback on the flow ... and length ... we can ... cut it down and it's ... one step" — "these grips are me really kind of letting the cement dry ... pretty quickly" but "field pieces are a little bit more chaotic" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).

**What he lost leaving institutions:** "One of the big things I lost when I left an institution was having like an editor and a guide over me ... my wife is who's also the leader of the company and ... executive producer ... looks at scripts and gives feedback on Flow Clarity ... but we don't have a robust editorial" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM) — wife as sole editorial feedback is claimed, not verified.

## 10. Editing rhythm — what he does show frame-by-frame

The most granular editing he documents is maps. Three videos triangulate one workflow:

**Current high-efficiency (Geolayers $200 plugin)** — long tutorial in [How I Make My Maps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GsojLuJpe_0):

- Two layers never animated directly: "map ... baseline ... you're never going to animate the position or scale" and "anchor ... you parent anything you want to stick on the map ... to this and it will stick"

## 11. Cadence and replication — what sacred, what sold

**Writing blocks are the religion** [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM):

> "my entire world is framed around a writing block from 9 to 2:00 p.m. ... that is my entire life no there's no meetings ... no slack ... no email ... no nothing between 9:00 and 2 p.m. usually three days a week sometimes four that is where it all gets done and those are sacred ... the whole system works if I can hit those blocks"

Vibe: "it's a little Voodoo magicky ... I wake up ... already know what the block is ... excited ... nervous ... belly ... churning ... I get ready and then as 9:00 comes I'm ... like game day ... I'm about to go out into the field ... in the locker room ... there's a lot of coffee ... 10-minute ramp up and then I jump in and there's music ... sometimes ... our composer makes ... giant Bank ... sometimes ... synth wave ... very wired in locked in obsessive ... talking to myself ... manic ... wrestling ... but it's flow"

**Plotted cadence now:** 30/year (down from 48), ~28 min average ⇒ ~15 min/week (interviewer math Harris confirms "yeah") [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM); "plotted ... 6 months in advance ... well oiled machine" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM). Interviewer frames 30 × ~20 min; Harris corrects to 28 min average [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM). Evidence JSON shows 2026 uploads roughly monthly (Aug 13, Jul 30, Jul 16, Jun 18, May 23 ...) consistent but not proving 6-month slate is locked — claimed.

**Sprint history and why they stopped weekly:** "when we were scaling the business and ... elevated it to become a professional organization but it hit a limit where we tried to optimize and get and and delegate and we were making a video a week we were making four videos a month and we had 17 videos going and it was like a well-oiled machine and we learned that that slowly started to suck the magic out of me I started to hate it because I was ... manufacturing my passion ... we dial back and ... embrace the fact that I'm going to be a bottleneck like I am a bottleneck because if I'm obsessed with it then it will ... have some magic" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM). Ceiling accepted; replication now is bottling the org for other channels.

**Current sustainable experiment:** "we've experimented with Sprint Sprint Sprint take ... a couple weeks of chill lately we've been experimenting with like three weeks of writing blocks and then one week of ... catch up and whatever and ... that will sometimes get eaten up" plus big rests "vacation ... spring break ... summer we usually take a big break ... December we usually take 3 weeks off" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM). Prior era "three-day blocks ... back then ... three-day Sprints ... we were on the treadmill and boy we got good real fast constraints constraints sharpen you" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).

**Verified vs claimed:** 17 videos in flight, 48 vs 30, 3-day vs 4-day blocks, December 3 weeks off — self-reported, not audited. The treadmill-sharpens claim is philosophy, not measured velocity.

## 12. Replication without burnout — constraints, not wellness docs

Harris never publishes a sleep/exercise/health SOP. His burnout answer is structural + attitudinal:

- **Constraints as sharpening, not crushing:** "constraints constraints sharpen you and ... so much of our skill as a team is because we ... have those constraints and that urgency we can't be precious ... constraints ... they've added way more value than they've created frustration" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM); dangerous year was 4/month with 17 in flight — he hated manufactured passion — so they imposed ceiling at 30 [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).
- **Accept the bottleneck:** "I have to feel it in my belly or ... I quickly get drained but when I love this ... I have this sort of Eternal energy ... not Eternal it ... wears me down but ... ## 15. In his own words — compressed replication checklist

1. **Run story day → greenlight in Slack + reporting brief** — "I Greenlight it ... I deliver a reporting brief ... El Chapo's tunnels" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM); researcher 3-4 weeks to 60-80 page info doc.
2. **Define the Billboard early, don't let it define the story** — 2-3 packagings, "does this have legs ... clickable" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM); 7 titles how/why promise, comps on one-of-10/ViewStats, AB test different directions (map vs Illuminati) [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).
3. **Make the promise with reinforcement in first minute, payoff at the end** — "I have to reinforce the promise ... within the first minute ... I don't answer it in first 10 seconds or first minute but by the end I have to" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM); vegetable-folding lets thumbnail 1M-click audience forget the hook while learning resource curse [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).
4. **Write by coding — sentence = row = motion** — left prose right feeling, alt-a red checkbox, color codes without mouse, annotations — "every sentence or two has a new row" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM); green citations after fact-check 3-4 sources per assertion into public source dock [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).
5. **Build the stage as agents** — "loading up the icon with motives ... Mormons ... federal government ... slowly moving West ... tension ... anticipation" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM); who did what to whom (Pinker Bible) not migration-of noun [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).
6. **Open with action + invitation, not thesis** — "it started with the murder ..." gunshot/slow-mo [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM); "I want to show you what this map says" equalizing friend framing + circling directing eye [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM); let "there's a lot more going on" live implicitly as foreshadowing retention.
7. **Switch voices to pace** — explainer play-by-play ↔ contemplative poetic (undetonated bombs / abandoned bones) [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM); Economist palace coffee human detail inside rigor [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).
8. **Pendulum the field** — don't over-engineer standup takes on ground; gather experiential reacting/character, then "write the pros word for word ... in the vo booth" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM); field notes via phone transcription wrestling after each interview [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).
9. **Fresh art, same vernacular** — no channel branding package, new art direction per video with color/palette/title templates/signs/collage/set/clutter [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM); write vibe in art-ingredients tab, screenshots/Pinterest, visual producer Alex → mood board [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM); rough animate in AE with shape layers over satellite to time with music before Premiere [The secrets behind my animations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FvddujhKRa0); map via Photoshop/Illustrator/AE/Premiere + Geolayers anchored null or vintage wand/stamp/pen path [How I Make My Maps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GsojLuJpe_0) [How Johnny Harris makes his maps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1sWy42yq6Jg).
10. **Make sound the instrument** — full-time composer Tom Fox since 2018, every video custom tone (Europe / Putin / Doritos) [Why I Hired A Music Composer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_vCfYVtXJgs).
11. **Work in sacred blocks, protect the ceiling** — 9am-2pm blocks "sacred ... no meetings ... no slack" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM); 3 weeks writing + 1 week catch-up seasonality + December 3 weeks / summer big break [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM); ceiling 30 not 48 because "I'm going to be a bottleneck ... if I'm obsessed ... magic" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM) — replication is bottling org for other creator-led channels, not cloning his throughput.
12. **Find reps through care, not template** — "wrestling with animation and Photoshop and illustrator ... Gateway ... to become better writer" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM); "reps and Reps and Reps ... terrible ... through that" [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM); next frontier is inviting human characters to carry the story beyond his eyes [David Perell transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zq4b96m1AvM).
