# Source Library — Deep-Documented Long-Form Video Workflows

**Purpose:** Every documentation source for the 15 shortlisted creators, organized per creator, with provenance and currency flags. This is the register behind the [README](README.md) ranking and the [recurring patterns](recurring-patterns.md) analysis.

**How to read the flags:**

- **FIRST-PARTY** = the creator's own words/production (own channel, own site, or the creator speaking as a verified guest). **SECOND-HAND** = reporting, summaries, or synthesis by someone else (used only for corroboration, per the run's evidence rules).
- **MONETIZED** = the doc carries sponsorships, paywalls, course pricing, or marketing intent — treat self-reported claims as marketing-adjacent. **INDEPENDENT** = no direct monetization of the doc itself.
- **Still current as of 2026?** — YES / NO / UNCLEAR + one-line reason, carried over from the Phase 1.5 shortlist recency notes and the case studies where those went further.

**Completeness note:** 12 of 15 creators have completed case studies; their entries below are pulled from the case studies' source links. **tom-scott, colin-and-samir, and matt-davella have no case study** (Phase 2 closed early after repeated subagent crashes) — their entries carry only shortlist metadata and are flagged `case study not completed — metadata from shortlist only`. They appear in no pattern table.
