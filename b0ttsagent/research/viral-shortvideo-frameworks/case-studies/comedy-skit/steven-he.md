## §2 Workflow (deep narrative)

### 2.1 Creator-study / ideation — the "Study" phase (his core advice: "Study")

- **Hundreds of hours of study before the first upload.** "Right off the bat, I spent hundreds of hours just studying before I even made a video" — studying the algorithm and his own numbers, powered by a maths orientation he calls "a massive edge" [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. Same figure repeated: "a couple hundred hours studying the algorithm" before his first TikTok [TUBEFILTER 2022-03 | 2026: YES], and "about a couple hundred hours studying the social media platforms" [NETINF 2024-08 | 2026: YES]. **Cross-corroborated ×3 → VERIFIED.**
- **Channel-dissection method.** He studied entire catalogs: "I would look at their entire channel... their first 20 videos did not pop up, why their 21st video popped off. What is it about that video that made it pop off?" — then tracked subsequent ups/downs [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. Advice to new creators: study ~100 channels — "you can do that in like a month" — after which your algorithm understanding "will be far far greater than the majority of starter YouTubers" [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. Influences studied: KSI, Ryan Higa, Smosh, Nathan Doan, RDC World, Caleb City, Key & Peele, Chappelle's Show, Sidemen, PewDiePie [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES].
- **TikTok-era study (2020):** "after studying TikTok for a couple hundred hours, it was pretty clear that the videos that do well... are comedy" → chose comedy deliberately despite being a dramatic actor ("I came from the opposite angle of what can I offer you?") [TUBEFILTER 2022-03 | 2026: YES].
- **Research-first ideation, not self-expression.** "Every video begins with research instead of coming from my brain" [NETINF 2024-08 | 2026: YES]. Concrete method: fresh/incognito browser on YouTube.com so his own history doesn't skew recommendations — "judging by the things that pop up in front of you, those would be the things that have the extraordinarily high interest" [CREATORHB 2023-10 | 2026: YES]. Trend-anchored example: a big iPhone launch "gave me the cue that people were interested in this topic... that is when I apply my creativity on making the iPhone 15 launch funny" [NETINF 2024-08 | 2026: YES].
- **Audience-first doctrine:** "It's more important to me what the audience wants to see than what I want to make. I'm not here to express, I'm here to deliver" [FORBES 2022-04 | 2026: YES — restated in 2025 as "a professional delivers"] [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. Relatability engineering: jokes written so they're "not just limited to an Asian culture, but relatable to as many cultures as possible" (discovered after "Why Asians pack so much"-type videos drew comments from Hispanic grandmothers and Greek teenagers) [TUBEFILTER 2022-03 | 2026: YES].
- **Probability mindset (anti-luck):** "I always choose probability over luck because you have power over probability" — after 3,000+ acting rejections; lottery math: 1M tickets ≈65% win, 2M ≈83%, 3M ≈98.5% [POPCULT 2023-05 | 2026: YES — repeated in 2025 as "10 failures, 15 failures... extract as much value as possible"] [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. Company named **Failure Management Productions** ("I wear that proudly... it's the name of my company") [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES], after "getting rejected 3,000+ times as an actor and making 200 videos that nobody watched" [CREATORHB 2023-10 | 2026: YES]. **Failure Management doctrine corroborated ×2 → VERIFIED.**

### 2.2 Format systems — a portfolio of product lines

- **The core unit: 2–3 minute sketch.** "My format is usually a comedy sketch that lasts about two minutes. From zero to two minutes is the actual skit... the integration from two minutes to three minutes, and then after three minutes, I would add other content like bloopers, extra jokes or just me talking to the audience" — sponsor integration deliberately trapped inside the watch window [CREATORHB 2023-10 | 2026: YES]. **CLAIMED (single-source).**
- **Why 3 minutes:** "a sketch dies after three minutes... it's pretty common knowledge that you can run a comedic angle about 3 minutes before it starts dying" — lineage cited: Monty Python, Chappelle's Show, SNL, Key & Peele [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. Two sketches can be stitched for 6–8min if desired [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. **CLAIMED.**
- **Short length as a CTR weapon:** lower-commitment videos get clicked more ("if it's like a 20-minute science video, you're like 'Ah, I don't have 20 minutes'"... "I made a gamble of consistently and exclusively posting short sketches... around two minutes... sacrifice watch time") — the decision he credits for reaching 3M subs in two years [TUBEFILTER 2022-03 | 2026: YES]. During the pandemic "my clickthrough was really really high compared to 5 minute 10 minute 15-minute videos" [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES].
- **Format-evolution arc:** TikTok single-joke 10-second videos (2020) → ~1 minute → YouTube sketches of 1–2 minutes → 2-minute-plus sketches [TUBEFILTER 2022-03 | 2026: YES].
- **Long-form/shorter hybrid, both-format workflow:** "I'll make a skit. I'll make it into both formats: long form and then I'll cut it down into a vertical short form" [POPCULT 2023-05 | 2026: YES]; "I'll make everything for YouTube, and then I'll cut snippets of it off or I'll change it into vertical and post it on TikTok" [TUBEFILTER 2022-03 | 2026: YES]. **Corroborated ×2 → VERIFIED.** (Note: the mission tag "Shorts-first→long-form hybrid" is contradicted — see §5.)
- **Long-form satellites:** 12-minute scripted series *Ginormo!* (6 episodes, kaiju parody, with Ken Mok) [POPCULT 2023-05 | 2026: UNCLEAR — 2023 project, launched May 2023, flopped] [CREATORHB 2023-10 | 2026: YES]; 30-minute episodic improv show *Fishbowl* (Shark Tank parody — "we're not trying to win, we're trying to make the judges laugh") doing 5–6M views per 30-min upload at the top end [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. **CLAIMED (single-source).**
- **Four-to-five running "systems"** (2025): three-minute sketch system, 30-min episodic, a short-form system ("makes shorts despite how much I don't like them"), in-real-life content, facecam format — each engineered to profitability then delegated ("for that one, I only show up like an hour for a 10-12 minute video and then that runs on its own... monthly profit") [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. **CLAIMED.**
- **Platform-period strategy:** "I'll take periods where I will target a platform to serve a purpose" — TikTok for initial exposure, YouTube to build community, a six-month strategy specifically for YouTube Shorts, Reels play planned for H1-2025: "I will dedicate the first half of 2025 to this play" [NETINF 2024-08 | 2026: UNCLEAR — Reels outcome not tracked in this corpus].
- **The one KPI:** "There is only one number I care about in everything I do and that is views per upload long form... I don't care about subscribers... I really don't care about shorts views at all" — because long-form views are "intentional" [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. **CLAIMED (single-source, but consistent with his 2022–2024 statements about long-form primacy).**

### 2.3 Hook

- **"I usually have my biggest joke in the first seven seconds"** [CREATORHB 2023-10 | 2026: YES]. Then "seven, eight seconds to set up for another punch line," cycling down a curated joke list [CREATORHB 2023-10 | 2026: YES]. **CLAIMED (single-source; no independent hook-timing claim elsewhere).**
- **Thumbnail + title as comedic hook:** "I would be the kind to try and deliver a laugh in the thumbnail. I will try to deliver a laugh in the few words I have in the title, and then punch it with the thumbnail" — explicitly *rejecting* the vague-title curiosity style (MoistCr1TiKaL cited as the counter-example) [CREATORHB 2023-10 | 2026: YES]. **CLAIMED.**
- **The character/catchphrase as pattern-interrupt:** "E-MO-shun-al DAMM-age!" anchored a lexicon ("Failure!", "What the HAIL," "Beijing Corn", "I will send you to Jesus!") [FORBES 2022-04 | 2026: YES — "Emotional damage" still his calling card in 2025 per Driven/RTÉ]. **VERIFIED (Forbes + RTÉ + Driven all reference the catchphrase's continuing currency).**
- **Diagnostic hook standard:** a retention drop "in the first five seconds, maybe from 100% to like 50% was a clear red flag" [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]; in the 12-minute format context: "within 30 seconds, 20% of people have dropped off" [POPCULT 2023-05 | 2026: YES]. **First-frame timing itself: no source → N/A.**

### 2.4 Production

- **Solo, amateur-grade, deliberate.** "I'm the one that presses the record button... I'm the one that sets up my mic and my costume"; "perhaps one of the biggest channels at my... unproduced production level" [CREATORHB 2023-10 | 2026: YES]. Low production value is a *feature*: "you don't have a prop weapon, use a banana... that goofiness was a huge strength" [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. Fans 100% preferred his old overexposed, out-of-focus quality to the crisp new look — "100% of the time" [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES].
- **Two-hour multi-character recording:** records all lines for each character at once, switches camera angle and costume for the next character; transfers footage to editor; ~1 week end-to-end per video, writing being the hardest part [CREATORHB 2023-10 | 2026: YES].
- **Equipment escalation then ROI-based retreat:** iPhone (first ~220 videos — "I didn't have a camera and I made 220 videos" [TUBEFILTER 2022-03 | 2026: YES]) → Sony a6400 (first-ever check, ~$1,000, spent entirely on the camera [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES] [TUBEFILTER 2022-03 | 2026: YES]) → FX3 → FX6 → RED Komodo → RED Raptor → **back down to FX3** because Raptor's no-autofocus/60s power-on/battery drain cost more crew time than the "2% better color science" returns in views [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES].
- **Cost-engineering ("Does this return me money? Kill it"):** killed the lighting department (high-key flat lighting needs no moving lights — "I pre-light the thing and I just tell the lighting person to leave the set"), killed the focus-puller role, lighter sets [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. Budget philosophy shift via MrBeast (weeks before the episode): "it's not that scaling budget does not scale results. It's that you haven't found the right thing to spend it on" → now hunting ROI-scalable elements: **action choreography** (top-3 performers of 2025 were action sketches; hired an action director; breakaway tables — "Fighting for the Bill") and **collaborations** (historically few; testing bigger talent) [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. **CLAIMED.**
- **High-end exception:** *Ginormo!* shoots ran ~100 person-days — "to earn that revenue back is significantly more difficult than me shooting by myself, which is free" [POPCULT 2023-05 | 2026: YES].
- **One shoot → many products:** a one-day, 15-person shoot produced three comedy skits and (with earlier collab shoots) hit trending #6, #7, and #1 simultaneously in the US — "how to turn one shoot into multiple things" [POPCULT 2023-05 | 2026: YES]. **CLAIMED.**
- **Performance craft as a retention tool:** "I would land a laugh with simply a lens" [NETINF 2024-08 | 2026: YES]; "I can use this camera movement to land this laugh" — constant per-video innovation as doctrine [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES].

### 2.5 Retention engineering

- **Optimization loop (his "failure management" process):** upload → identify what didn't work (e.g., first-5-second retention cliff) → generate 5 solution ideas (own thinking + "what did this channel do? What did that channel do?") → try them across 20–30 videos → the one that works "I would take that and I would put it in my quiver" [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. "By video number 50, 60, 100... that's when you have a solid product" [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. **VERIFIED ×2 (same doctrine in CREATORHB: "you have to make 100 videos and they're probably not going to work out... by the time you have 300 videos, maybe one is good").**
- **Explicit algorithm targets:** "90% of our job is understanding the algorithm and optimizing for it... I need to get my click-through from six percent to seven percent... I need my retention to go from 60% to 80%. I need my average watch time to hit about fifteen seconds" [POPCULT 2023-05 | 2026: UNCLEAR — specific numbers are 2023 channel-state; targets re-stated differently in Oct 2023, see §5]. Retention 60→80% repeated verbatim: "How do I get click-throughs from 9 percent to 15 percent? How do I get retention from 60 to 80 percent?" [CREATORHB 2023-10 | 2026: UNCLEAR]. **Retention target VERIFIED ×2; CTR numbers CONTRADICTORY (6→7 vs 9→15).**
- **Joke-density retention:** ~30 jokes per video across "a verbal punchline to an audio or visual cue to performance, props or even frame composition," curated, ordered, and "woven... with narrative" [CREATORHB 2023-10 | 2026: YES]. **CLAIMED.**
- **Data access advantage:** "now I chat with literally the team at Google that codes the algorithm... I have his phone number"; plus a peer circle of top YouTubers exchanging learnings every couple of weeks ("here's what I'm doing for the next five videos. What do you think?") [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. **CLAIMED.**
- **Early-warning red flags:** 5-second retention cliff; 20% drop-off within 30 seconds forced different writing for 12-minute format ("If you wrote traditionally... you would take two minutes to develop a plot and tell the payoff in the third minute. We don't have that luxury") [POPCULT 2023-05 | 2026: YES].
- **Micro-format numbers (Shorts system):** "a short with an average watch time of 50 seconds is all but guaranteed to reach five million views" [CREATORHB 2023-10 | 2026: UNCLEAR — 2023 claim, no later confirmation]. **CLAIMED.**

### 2.8 Channel-crash recovery (the 80% crash)

- **The event:** a show he spent two years and "multiple six figures" of personal money on — 12-minute scripted narrative episodes, different genre, new characters, no famous punchlines — "performed maybe 10% of my regular uploads and it also dragged every other upload down to about 20% of what we did at the time... it tanked the entire company, not just itself" [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. This is *Ginormo!* — cross-corroborated: Creator Handbook (Oct 2023) describes "11 months producing a scripted narrative with a cast full of my best friends and it completely flopped. The performance, frankly, was the worst the channel had seen in about two years"; Pop-Culturalist (May 2023) confirms the 12-minute, 6-episode format [CREATORHB 2023-10 | 2026: YES] [POPCULT 2023-05 | 2026: YES]. **VERIFIED ×2 (implicitly ×3).**
- **The post-mortem (6 months of study):** "I asked YouTubers, I asked the audience, I asked consultants... the audience subscribed for the three minute Stephen comedy sketch. That's why they clicked in every day. And I delivered a product that was entirely different expecting the same result" — the Apple/avocado-toast metaphor [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. **CLAIMED (single-source interpretation).**
- **The recovery doctrine — 10% pivots:** "I would do 10% pivots... I have to still be me and deliver the Stephen He video experience... give them like a 10% addition of something rather than taking away" — add 10% runtime, or cast, or writing; try 10 directions; "if I'm lucky, one of them will work. And that's where I lean in further" [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. **CLAIMED.**
- **Recovery results:** the 30-minute episodic system (Fishbowl) succeeded — 5–6M views at the top, 1–1.5M for the lower ones [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]; return to core sketch system; 2025 growth investments (action, collabs) [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES]. **CLAIMED.**
- **Earlier volatility survived:** pre-crash "hype" cycles dropped 90% (1.5M/day → ~100K) [TUBEFILTER 2022-03 | 2026: YES]; "Ad-pocalypses" — YouTube incentive-structure shifts cutting views/engagement/revenue "as much as 90%" [FORBES 2022-04 | 2026: YES]. **VERIFIED ×2 (distinct events, same pattern).**
- **Psychology of recovery:** early-career metric obsession ("every week that it was down, I'd be depressed") matured into "I understand the larger journey rather than the in-the-moment" [NETINF 2024-08 | 2026: YES]; risk aversion as stability engine — "I don't take on a lot of risk... I don't spend much money" [NETINF 2024-08 | 2026: YES], "I generally like to experiment on things that are either just my time... I personally stay away from what I did with that show" [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES].
- **The Emotional Damage origin (the format that survived everything):** shot by his mother in a park/field in Ireland (visa renewal wait), gaming ragdoll-fall footage, rolling in mud for hours on the A6400; he wanted to skip day two ("Mom, I'm feeling a bit tired") — she made him finish: "you're going to do it today"; the clip "would be watched over 7 billion times" (host's figure, unchallenged by Steven) [DRIVEN 2025-12 | 2026: YES] [CREATORHB 2023-10 | 2026: YES — fever, "man up"] [TUBEFILTER 2022-03 | 2026: YES — mom operated camera]. **Origin story VERIFIED ×3; the "7 billion" figure is a single-source host claim — flag.** Related: 2B TikTok views of the meme in 2 months [TUBEFILTER 2022-03 | 2026: YES]; "I don't make a single dollar out of the actual meme itself" [YT-BLOG 2024-01 | 2026: UNCLEAR — direct meme licensing not tracked].

## §5 Caveats & Contradictions

1. **CTR target contradiction (explicit):** Pop-Culturalist (May 2023): "click-through from six percent to seven percent". Creator Handbook (Oct 2023): "click-throughs from 9 percent to 15 percent". Same retention phrase (60→80%) in both. Either channel state changed within 5 months, or he cites ranges loosely. Flag: both figures are single-source; report both, treat exact numbers as illustrative rather than prescriptive.
2. **Mission-tag correction:** the brief's tag "TikTok→YouTube Shorts-first→long-form hybrid" does not match his own chronology. His sequence: TikTok-first (10s single-joke videos, 2020) → **YouTube long-form-first** (~2min sketches; "I'll make everything for YouTube, and then I'll cut snippets... for TikTok") → Shorts as a *derivative system* ("a short form system that makes shorts despite how much I don't like them") → platform-period plays (Shorts strategy, Reels H1-2025). He is long-form-first, not Shorts-first [TUBEFILTER 2022-03] [POPCULT 2023-05] [DRIVEN 2025-12] [NETINF 2024-08].
3. **Shorts hatred vs Shorts participation (stated tension):** "I can't stand shorts... I hate SHORTS SO MUCH" while simultaneously running a Shorts system and exploiting 60× reach; he calls himself a hypocrite about it [DRIVEN 2025-12]. Not a factual contradiction, but material for interpretation of his platform-specific mechanics.
4. **"Impossible to predict success" vs systematic targeting:** he insists "I am the absolute worst at predicting success" (90s skit's 60s cut = most-viewed Short; Emotional Damage performed below average for months) [CREATORHB 2023-10] [POPCULT 2023-05], yet his entire framework is predictive optimization (CTR/retention targets, research-first ideation). Resolution from his own words: optimization raises hit *probability* ("probability over luck"), not certainty [DRIVEN 2025-12] [POPCULT 2023-05].
5. **Emotional Damage viral-window timing:** Tubefilter (Mar 2022) dates the surge to Dec 2021–Feb 2022 ("2 billion views in 2 months on TikTok"); Doctor Mike's episode description (Feb 2023) says "meteoric viral rise in the summer of 2022". Likely both true (staged spread) but sources disagree on peak; flag.
6. **Date discrepancy:** lockdown/held-commercial date given as March 20, 2020 in the studio segment and March 30, 2020 in the car segment of the same episode [DRIVEN 2025-12]. NYC lockdown was Mar 20, 2020; treat Mar 20 as correct.
7. **Channel-creation date:** YouTube stats (in Creator Handbook) say channel created Mar 3, 2019, but he states he created the channel only after 1M TikTok followers (post-Mar-2020) and "should have created it at the very beginning" [TUBEFILTER 2022-03] [CREATORHB 2023-10]. Account-registration vs active-posting distinction; minor.
8. **View totals:** RTÉ (Mar 2025): 3.6B views / 13M subs; Net Influencer (Aug 2024): "12 million subscribers or 5 billion views" (his own words); shortlist VIEWDATA: 13M subs/3.6B views. Consistent with growth; exact totals depend on date — flag as snapshot figures.

Consistent with audience-first doctrine but worth flagging as a public-position shift.
