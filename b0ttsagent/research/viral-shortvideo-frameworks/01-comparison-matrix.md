# Cross-Creator Comparison Matrix

**Purpose:** fast lookup — compare any two of the 10 documented viral-short-video workflows element by element. Every row is compressed; open the linked case study for the full chain with source URLs and per-field citations.

**Reader task:** *find / compare*. Structure follows the [Extraction Schema](../../../../C:/Obsidian/Repos/Main/Personal/Personal/Development/Prompts/Workspace/2026/Short%20Content%20Research%20V4.md) from the mission spec — the schema's closed-vocabulary axes are the columns; out-of-vocabulary mechanisms are merged in only where ≥2 case studies independently surface them (per the spec's `## New Terms` path), and they carry their own normalized column name.

**Reading key:**
- Slug legend — `zk`=Zach King · `mb`=MrBeast · `dm`=Dhar Mann · `kl`=Keith Lee · `ar`=Airrack · `sh`=Steven He · `cs`=Caleb Simpson · `nd`=Nick DiGiovanni · `ss`=Sam Sulek · `jh`=Jenny Hoyos.
- Each cell ends with `[slug]` pointing to the case study file (`case-studies/<category>/<slug>.md`) where that field's per-source citation chain lives.
- **N/A** = source-set documents no claim for that field; **N/A-reject** = creator explicitly *rejects* a vocabulary mechanism (a verified finding, not a gap); **INFERRED** = field value is implied by stated rules but no source names the exact frame/beat (case study flags the inference).
- Dominance score formula: `0.5 × hit_rate + 0.3 × hit_magnitude + 0.2 × activity`, `hit_magnitude` capped at 1.0; reproducible from the dominance inputs in the README.
