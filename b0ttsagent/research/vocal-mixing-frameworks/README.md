# Vocal Mixing Frameworks — Research Index

**What this is:** 34 evidence-cited case studies on how verified, mainstream-relevant (2021–2026) engineers, producers, and artists record and mix vocals — weighted ~70% hip-hop/pop, 30% rock/alt — plus a synthesis layer that distills the cross-artist formulas.

**Built:** 2026-08-05, by 5 parallel research subagents, each forced to cite a source URL for every process claim. Research brief: repeatable, deeply documented workflows that demonstrably worked on hit records — not vibes.

**Read this first if you only read three things:**
1. `02-recurring-patterns.md` — what all the legends share (the actual "formula")
2. `03-universal-vocal-chain.md` — the distilled starter chain, translated to free tools
3. `case-studies/01-modern-rap-mix/bainz.md` and `case-studies/02-new-wave-rap/kesha-lee.md` — the two most fully documented rap vocal chains in existence

## Evidence tiers (used in every case study)

- **A** — primary long-form technical sources: Sound on Sound *Inside Track*, Mix with the Masters, Pensado's Place, Puremix breakdowns, Tape Notes, the person's own interviews/courses
- **B** — reputable secondary: credible writeups, podcast appearances, vendor artist interviews (Antares, UA, Waves)
- **C** — community reconstruction / fan reverse-engineering — always labeled, never treated as fact

## Corrections the research surfaced (assumptions vs. verified credits)

- **Kendrick Lamar's *GNX* (2024) was mixed by Oli Jacobs, not MixedByAli.** Ali's verified recent work is *Mr. Morale & The Big Steppers* (2022) and SZA tracks.
- **twenty one pilots' *Clancy* (2024) was mixed by Adam Hawkins, not Neal Avron.**
- **Billie Eilish's *Hit Me Hard and Soft* (2024) was mixed by Jon Castelli & Aron Forbes, not Rob Kinelski** (Kinelski mixed everything before it).
- **Louis Bell has no verified Sabrina Carpenter credits** — her *Short n' Sweet* producers were Bunetta/John Ryan/Kirkpatrick/Antonoff (Kirkpatrick and Serban Ghenea do appear there).
- **Carti's *MUSIC* (2025) was mixed mostly by Fritz Owens** (all 30 tracks), with Mike Dean on one track — not by any of the names commonly guessed online.

## Honest limitations

- **The underground lane is thin by nature.** Yeat, wifiskeleton, Drain Gang, and the Opium engineers have published almost no technical documentation. Their files separate verified fact (credits, gear photos, workflow behavior) from community reconstruction (labeled C-tier). If a blog gives you exact "Yeat vocal settings," no primary source confirms them.
- **Some canonical interviews predate 2021** (e.g., Kesha Lee's SoS breakdown, Manny Marroquin's 'Stronger' chain). They were included only where the person is still mainstream-active and the technique is still their documented method.
- Paywalled video (MWTM, Puremix, NTM) is cited from session pages, official summaries, and community Q&As answered by the engineers themselves — settings quoted from those are marked as such inside each file.
