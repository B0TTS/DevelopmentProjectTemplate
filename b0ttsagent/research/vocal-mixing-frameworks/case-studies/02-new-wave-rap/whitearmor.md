## Vocal Philosophy
- **Auto-Tune IS the sound — documented by Bladee:** "I hated to hear my voice without the Auto-Tune. It's how we found our sound. Without it we wouldn't have committed to doing it – it sounded too bad." [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2024/nov/18/i-was-really-not-ok-bladee-on-ptsd-charli-xcx-and-being-struck-by-lightning)
- **The collective has been "playing around with Auto-Tune" since their teens**, freestyling in the vein of Lil B and Chief Keef; early tracks were "so digitally processed that they passed through the uncanny valley and ended up somewhere strangely melodic and emotive." [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2024/nov/18/i-was-really-not-ok-bladee-on-ptsd-charli-xcx-and-being-struck-by-lightning)
- **Melody above everything:** "Everything I've ever wanted to say has been in the melodies" / "I'm not an aesthetic person at all; I don't really care about clothes or visual stuff – I'm strictly melody." [Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/music/article/56278/1/whitearmor-drain-gang-music-for-weddings-interview)
- **Anti-interference workflow:** "I shut a lot of that stuff out to be able to focus and to have your own compass of what's good." [Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/music/article/56278/1/whitearmor-drain-gang-music-for-weddings-interview)

## The Vocal Chain (as documented)
**No chain has been published by whitearmor, Ecco2k, or Bladee.** Documented facts:
1. **Auto-Tune is foundational and omnipresent** — the group's entire identity formed around it in their teens; Bladee still describes his voice as unlistenable to him without it [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2024/nov/18/i-was-really-not-ok-bladee-on-ptsd-charli-xcx-and-being-struck-by-lightning).
2. **Self-contained production:** the collective makes, produces, and releases their own music (self-released *Cold Visions* after leaving Year0001; whitearmor produces; Ecco2k co-produces/mixes/designs) [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2024/nov/18/i-was-really-not-ok-bladee-on-ptsd-charli-xcx-and-being-struck-by-lightning) / [032c](https://magazine.032c.com/magazine/drain-gang).
3. **On-site creation:** *Crest* was made in a rented cottage by the sea ("The *Crest* album has a lot of inspiration in Abba and this Swedish movie with Björn Isfält") [Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/music/article/56278/1/whitearmor-drain-gang-music-for-weddings-interview); *Cold Visions* was "made in two weeks in his house in Stockholm" [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2024/nov/18/i-was-really-not-ok-bladee-on-ptsd-charli-xcx-and-being-struck-by-lightning).
4. **The sonic frame:** *Cold Visions* = "raging, blown-out trap beats" (many produced by F1lthy — see f1lthy.md for that documented aesthetic) [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2024/nov/18/i-was-really-not-ok-bladee-on-ptsd-charli-xcx-and-being-struck-by-lightning) / [severancetime Substack](https://severancetime.substack.com/p/mp3-as-instrument-notes-on-f1lthys).

**UNVERIFIED / community reconstruction (C-tier):** fan analyses claim FL Studio/Ableton usage, "angelic" layered Auto-Tuned stacks, and heavy FX sends — none confirmed by primary sources. Treat all of it as fan reverse-engineering.

## Recording / Tracking Side
- **Origin:** "The Drain Gang collective... have been best friends since their teens, playing around with Auto-Tune, and freestyling in the vein of idiosyncratic US rappers such as Lil B and Chief Keef." [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2024/nov/18/i-was-really-not-ok-bladee-on-ptsd-charli-xcx-and-being-struck-by-lightning)
- **Bladee records at home:** *Cold Visions* made in two weeks at his Stockholm house, self-released [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2024/nov/18/i-was-really-not-ok-bladee-on-ptsd-charli-xcx-and-being-struck-by-lightning)
- **Whitearmor's creative method:** "honing in on specific parts of songs and then reimagining a lot of stuff. I focused on what I didn't like a lot – a problem-based mindset." [Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/music/article/56278/1/whitearmor-drain-gang-music-for-weddings-interview)
- **Formative training:** drums as a teenager, indie-rock band, then electronic noise/experimental route; Atlanta rap mixtapes (Waka Flocka) as his "biggest music-related internet experiences." [Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/music/article/56278/1/whitearmor-drain-gang-music-for-weddings-interview)

## Signature Moves
1. **Auto-Tune as the instrument everyone sings through** — the whole collective's identity was built on the processed voice; the effect is not decoration but the group's actual voice. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2024/nov/18/i-was-really-not-ok-bladee-on-ptsd-charli-xcx-and-being-struck-by-lightning)
2. **Scene-of-creation albums** — renting a cottage (Crest), recording at home in two weeks (Cold Visions): the environment is a production decision. [Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/music/article/56278/1/whitearmor-drain-gang-music-for-weddings-interview) / [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2024/nov/18/i-was-really-not-ok-bladee-on-ptsd-charli-xcx-and-being-struck-by-lightning)
3. **"Problem-based mindset"** — writing from what you dislike in a reference, reimagining specific parts rather than copying wholes. [Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/music/article/56278/1/whitearmor-drain-gang-music-for-weddings-interview)
4. **Melody as the entire message** — "Everything I've ever wanted to say has been in the melodies"; production serves the melodic line above all. [Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/music/article/56278/1/whitearmor-drain-gang-music-for-weddings-interview)

## What To Steal
1. **Decide your voice needs the effect, and commit** — Bladee's documented logic: the processed voice IS the voice; mix into it, don't apologize for it. Free: MAutoPitch/Graillon 2 at moderate retune; sing through it while you record if your DAW allows live monitoring (Audacity: record dry, then commit the tuned version as your reference).
2. **Use the "problem-based mindset"** — take a song you love, identify exactly what you DON'T like about it, and rebuild those parts your way. That's whitearmor's documented writing method.
3. **Album-as-place** — change rooms for projects (cottage, home, studio) and let the acoustics/energy shape the vocal sound; even a bedroom vs. a living room changes your voice.
4. **Speed is a feature** — *Cold Visions*: 30 songs in two weeks at home, self-released. Set deadlines measured in days, not months.
5. **Melody over mixing** — before chasing chain perfection, make the melodic line strong enough that "everything you want to say" is in it.
6. **Stay self-contained** — no label pipeline, no external team: the whole Drain Gang production/mix/design chain is internal. For a bedroom artist this is the model, not a limitation.
