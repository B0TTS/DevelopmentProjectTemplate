## Vocal Philosophy
- **Everything is DIY and self-made:** began making music at 16 with friends (stated in his April 2025 &friends interview) [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wifiskeleton); his known catalog was made and released by himself — no external engineers/producers documented.
- **Old songs were made on BandLab:** his own Instagram caption on a remake video: "how i made 'they'll post it online' on bandlab - used to make all the old songs on bandlab" [Instagram reel (his account)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DVmeO5rEUUw/) — i.e., his foundational vocal production pipeline was a free browser/phone DAW.
- **Sound identity (as documented by critics):** "Enigmatic bedroom producer... part guitar-driven alt pop, part post-Soundcloud rap, part overblown digital hyperpop," depressive themes, lo-fi/glitchy textures [AllMusic](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/mn0004840338) / [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/us/music/news-who-wifiskeleton-real-name-artist-tributes-pour-official-discord-server-post-goes-viral)

**UNVERIFIED / community reconstruction (C-tier):** YouTube "how to sound like wifiskeleton" tutorials (e.g., "How to make Indie-Pop like Wifiskelton," [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8dqR0ntLeo8)) reconstruct his chain by ear as: heavy Auto-Tune with fast retune, layered vocal doubles, pitch-shifted "angel" stacks, saturation/bitcrush on the vocal bus, and very loud vocals over compressed guitar loops. ## What To Steal
1. **Start in the free DAW** — BandLab made a whole catalog that later defined an artist; the tool is not the ceiling. If you only have Audacity + a phone, you have more than he started with.
2. **One good LDC + one interface is a complete chain** — his entire documented professional setup is a single Soyuz FET condenser into a UA Volt; budget equivalents: any large-diaphragm condenser (even a $60-100 one) + any clean USB interface. (Volt 476 is $329; the point is the architecture: one mic, one interface, one laptop.)
3. **Layer your voice into chords** — the "angel stack" (pitch-shifted double stacks) is his signature texture per fan reconstruction; in Audacity: duplicate the take, pitch-shift +3/+5 semitones (or use formant changes), widen with delay, blend low under the lead.
4. **Personas per project** — when your voice feels boring, change the treatment (different tuning, different layering, different aliases) instead of changing the music.
5. **Publish your process** — his own tutorial video is now a primary artifact; teaching the sound forced him to systematize it.
6. **Accept the lo-fi contract** — his documented aesthetic is "overblown" and glitchy on purpose; if your bedroom recording is imperfect, treat the imperfection as the style rather than the failure.
