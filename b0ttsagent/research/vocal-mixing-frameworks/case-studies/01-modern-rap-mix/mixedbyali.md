## Vocal Philosophy
- Analog-first: *"you can't get that analogue sound in the box, period. You simply cannot recreate that sound with plug-ins."* He mixes on an SSL 4000 G+ because "when you are looking at a screen, you are looking at numbers. Whereas when you are on a board in analogue, you are working with your ears." [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-kendrick-lamars-pimp-butterfly)
- Vocals are instruments to manipulate for emotion: *"you can alter people's emotions with certain frequencies and sonic textures... I can add delays and reverbs and other crazy effects to music or vocals and give them extra emotion."* [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-kendrick-lamars-pimp-butterfly)
- Accountable craft: *"I work off this mind set that you're only as good as your last mix."* [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/mixedbyali-interview-8548107/)
- Per-artist vocal presets: *"I have a go-to, nowadays, for different artists, for different vocal tones."* [NAMM.org](https://www.namm.org/trade-show/learn-mixing-mixedbyali-namm-show)

## The Vocal Chain (as documented)
**Tracking (TPAB-era, documented chain):** Stephen Paul-modified Telefunken U47 → **Neve 1073** mic pre → **Tube-Tech CL1B** compressor (U67 on "Alright"; stacked EV RE20 + EV 666 on "For Free?"); sometimes Pultec EQP-1A3. Tracking plugins (because faster): Waves Renaissance Compressor, Metaflanger, De-Esser, SSL Channel, S1 Imager, Avid Air Chorus. [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-kendrick-lamars-pimp-butterfly)

**Mix vocal chain — Kendrick lead, "These Walls" (with actual settings):**
1. Waves **Renaissance Compressor** — manual + opto modes, **threshold all the way down to -18** — "if anything in the mid-range leaps out too much, it keeps that in check" (Kendrick has a raspy mid-range voice).
2. Waves **SSL Channel strip** — high/low-pass filtering only; all real vocal EQ on the desk.
3. Waves **S1 Imager** — opens/widens the mid-range voice so it sits on top ("like a blanket tied in a knot... you open it up and spread it").
4. Outboard: **EMT 250** reverb; **Empirical Labs Distressor** on the lead — "adds some grit and presence at the top... opening up the top end, making it crisp and allowing his vocals to cut through."
5. Guests: **Fairchild 670** parallel compression; AMS RMX16 reverb; SSL board EQ.
6. His mixes are "reverse engineering": plug-in FX used at tracking get replaced by desk EQ + outboard at mix (Eventide H3500 replaces Metaflanger/S1; Dbx 902 replaces Waves De-Esser; Lexicon 480L for reverb). [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-kendrick-lamars-pimp-butterfly)

**Modern vocal-shaping move (2020s, UAD SSL 4000E):** official Universal Audio feature — he uses the **UAD SSL 4000 E Channel Strip** on vocals: **boost ~8 kHz (bell curve, not shelf)** for presence and **dip ~300 Hz low-mid** to remove mud — "push the lead vocal upfront." [UA official video](https://www.facebook.com/universalaudioinc/videos/channel-strips-can-be-a-powerful-secret-weapon-and-a-true-one-stop-shop-mixedbya/1256652095647524/), [nound.io writeup](https://www.nound.io/l/5vtvnAjwm2G)

**"The Box" (Roddy Ricch, US #1, 2020) — documented workshop breakdown:** session = 3 record tracks (reference rough, his mix, master); he demonstrated **parallel compression on the 808 with the Little Labs Voice of God plugin** ("beef up the 808 without irrevocably altering the original track"); for Roddy's vocal he pulled up **Kramer Master Tape + Soundtoys Decapitator** to get the distorted doubled vocal sound ("Roddy's voice transmogrified into the distorted and demonic doubled vocal style"); the whole song is built from five sounds (808, snare, two hi-hats, sample) — "how to drop the bass in that mix so that it's the most prominent part of your mix." [one37pm workshop report](https://web.archive.org/web/2023/https://www.one37pm.com/music/mixedbyali-interview)

**Mix method:** spends ~80% of mix time on **one Auratone speaker in mono** — "Dre always told me that if I could get something to sound amazing on crappy speakers, it'll sound brilliant on normal speakers"; NS10s for edits; loud mains pass last; mixes take 1–2 days + a day living with the mix (car, boombox); prints via Lavry Gold A-D + half-inch tape (TPAB); 2-bus: SSL bus compressor + GML 8200 EQ. [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-kendrick-lamars-pimp-butterfly)

## Recording / Tracking Side
- Vocal recording chain: U47 (Stephen Paul mod) → Neve 1073 → Tube-Tech CL1B — "gives a great, fat, warm vocal sound, especially in conjunction with the U47."
- Bus rig for touring: Pro Tools HD rack, Sony C800G + Telefunken U47, Avalon mic pre.
- During tracking he applies "crazy" plug-in effects at Kendrick's request (flanging, panning, etc.), later replaced by hardware in the mix. [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-kendrick-lamars-pimp-butterfly)

## Signature Moves
1. **Mono-Auratone mixing (~80% of the time)** — define balances/panning value in mono before stereo. [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-kendrick-lamars-pimp-butterfly)
2. **Reverse-engineering:** deliberately swap tracking plugins for desk EQ + outboard at mix time (analog sound, "you can't cheapskate good work"). [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-kendrick-lamars-pimp-butterfly)
3. **UAD SSL 4000E vocal preset:** +8 kHz bell presence / −300 Hz mud (the modern Ali move, shown by UA). [UA video](https://www.facebook.com/universalaudioinc/videos/channel-strips-can-be-a-powerful-secret-weapon-and-a-true-one-stop-shop-mixedbya/1256652095647524/)
4. **808 parallel compression with VOG + tape/saturation on vocals** ("The Box": Kramer Master Tape + Decapitator) — build hugeness in parallel so the original stays intact. [one37pm](https://web.archive.org/web/2023/https://www.one37pm.com/music/mixedbyali-interview)
5. **Effect-driven vocal emotion:** delays/reverbs/width used narratively, not decoratively ("I want people to experience my mixes"). [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-kendrick-lamars-pimp-butterfly)

## What To Steal
1. Mix in mono on one small speaker most of the time (free: mono button on your master bus) — balances and "does the vocal cut" become obvious.
2. Vocal chain order that works: gentle opto compression (threshold low, small ratio) BEFORE EQ, then widen with a stereo imager, then an 8 kHz presence bell + 300 Hz cut (free: any stock EQ; imager: free Ozone Imager).
3. Parallel 808/bass trick: send the 808 to a bus, add sub-reinforcement (Little Labs VOG concept; free: duplicate + pitch-shift down an octave + low-pass, or a free subharmonic plugin) and blend underneath.
4. Tape-style saturation + distortion on vocal doubles (Kramer Master Tape / Decapitator concept; free: Chow Tape Model, Softube Saturation Knob).
5. Reference your rough and the artist's earlier mixes — "you're only as good as your last mix" — A/B constantly.
6. Give each vocal its own saved chain per artist ("go-to for different artists for different vocal tones").
7. Check mixes in the car / on a phone speaker before finishing.
