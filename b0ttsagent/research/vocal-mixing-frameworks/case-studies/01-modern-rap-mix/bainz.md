## Vocal Philosophy
- The vocal sound starts in the room and the chain: "You work with what you get. Quite often I have to do a lot of clip gaining if the engineer wasn't very detail oriented during the tracking. **The mic and preamp are the most important part.**" [Live with Matt Rad #77](https://www.livewithmattrad.com/episodes/77)
- Vocals need at least some processing always, but the clean-up and the tone-shaping are separate jobs: "It depends on the way it's recorded to be honest but I always have to do at least some level of eq, compression and coloring." [MWTM community](https://community.mwtm.com/t/vocals-processing-method/4206)
- The mix must serve the artist's moment: Atlanta rappers improvise in the control room, "our job is to capture that, maintain sonic integrity and make the end result sound as amazing as possible." [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-young-thug-gunna-ski)
- Be an artist-pleaser but push back with taste: "Thug pushed me to do things I would not normally do, and now many people say that mix sounds amazing and they are using it as a mix reference." [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-young-thug-gunna-ski)

## The Vocal Chain (as documented)
**Recording chain (fixed, LA):** Sony C800 → **Neve 1073** → **Tube-Tech CL-1B**. (Atlanta SSL room: C800G → 1073 → dbx 902 de-esser → Summit Audio TLA-100.) *"I would not in any case use an SSL as my main vocal preamp, because I don't like the sound of an SSL on vocals."* [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-young-thug-gunna-ski)

**Recording template (speed/low-latency, Pro Tools HDX):** two record tracks with **Antares AutoTune** on the insert (he uses Auto-Tune on pretty much every track), playlist track, vocal audio tracks, ~a dozen aux FX tracks. Clip Effects (Opt+6, HDX-only) presets for instant harshness notches; iZotope RX for de-click/de-plosive/de-clip; Elgato Stream Deck XL + Soundflow macros. [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-young-thug-gunna-ski), [Matt Rad #51](https://www.livewithmattrad.com/episodes/51), [Antares](https://www.antarestech.com/blog/bainz-interview-2023-grammys)

**Mix vocal chain — Young Thug "Ski" (2021, exact insert order, documented):**
1. Lead track inserts: **FabFilter Pro-Q 3** → **AutoTune Pro** → **Tokyo Dawn Labs DeEdger (x2)** → **Oeksound Soothe 2** → **Oeksound Spiff** → **Pro-Q 3** → **Waves C6** → **SPL De-Esser** → **UAD Tube-Tech CL-1BN**. ("There's a lot of clean-up from plug-ins like the de-essers, Soothe and Spiff, but for the rest all these plug-ins are doing very minor things.")
2. Printed track below: **Kazrog True Iron** (transformer saturation) → **McDSP 404** multiband (takes 2dB in upper mids + some lows for plosives/proximity) → **Acustica Ruby 2** (DW Fearn VT-5 tube EQ emulation — "I get a lot of my vocal tone from the Ruby") → **Acustica Ultra Marine 4** (Fairchild emulation, "I often use on vocals") → **iZotope Neutron 3** ("I really like the Exciter in the Neutron and crank that up quite a bit"; two bands, warm mode — the only mode he uses).
3. Vocal bus ("Slime"): **FabFilter Pro-DS** de-esser → **McDSP Royal Mu** compressor → **Soundtheory Gullfoss** (dynamic EQ; "Don't overdo it. Only doing up to 20% on most projects") — all run through his **McDSP APB-16** analog hardware box.
4. **Sends (9 on the vocal bus):** outboard **Bricasti M7** plate reverb; outboard **Distressor** ("adds some more in-your-face up-front-ness... always check the phase"); **Eventide H910 Harmonizer** for stereo width; **Black Box HG-2** for saturation; **UAD Ocean Way** and **UAD Capitol Chambers** reverbs; hall reverb from tracking; **McDSP EC-300 quarter-note delay** with a **Waves RCompressor side-chained to the vocal** so the delay only sounds in the gaps. [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-young-thug-gunna-ski), [Matt Rad #51](https://www.livewithmattrad.com/episodes/51)
5. **Vox Throw folder (his signature framework):** ~15 audio effect tracks, each 100%-wet with its own plugin chain ("Manipulater x Blackhole", "Down low, monster voice", "Wide Chorused Double", "Wet, wide warm single verb repeat", "Short room Burst"...). For a big effect on one word/phrase he duplicates the audio, drags it onto a Throw track, mixes it in at low fader level. "I use all these different effects to make sure things don't get monotonous. It's how I keep excitement in the vocals and essentially it's what makes these rap vocals radio friendly. This is one of the last things I do in the entire process." [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-young-thug-gunna-ski)
6. **Mix bus (consistent):** Neve 5059 summing → SSL Fusion (drive knob) → Neve Portico master bus → Burl B2 A/D; on the bus track: McDSP Moo X, UAD SSL G bus, Brainworx Shadow Hills, Dangerous BAX EQ, UAD Chandler Curve Bender, iZotope Ozone 9; pre-master: AOM Invisible Limiter G2. Limiter never hits more than 1dB GR. [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-young-thug-gunna-ski), [Matt Rad #77](https://www.livewithmattrad.com/episodes/77)
7. **Template vs per-song:** recording template is fixed for speed; the mix chain is deliberately NOT a template — "the above vocal treatments are not part of a template. Every song is different. I cannot stress this enough." His consistent framework is: clean-up plugins on the audio tracks, tone-shaping plugins on the aux/bus ("I put the clean up stuff on audio tracks and then add tone shaping stuff to the bus"), commit when CPU gets heavy. [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-young-thug-gunna-ski), [MWTM community](https://community.mwtm.com/t/vocals-processing-method/4206)

**Gunna variant:** same vocal treatment but "might swap the Ruby 2 for the McDSP Royal Q EQ" on the main vocal. [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-young-thug-gunna-ski)

## Recording / Tracking Side
- Beat loaded as a 2-track into the Pro Tools recording template; rappers improvise; vocals often laid in under an hour; comps sometimes done by Thug himself in Pro Tools; vocals nudged back slightly for feel ("Almost every part gets nudged around"). [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-young-thug-gunna-ski)
- Records in the control room (live vibe) → heavy iZotope RX cleanup afterwards; Clip Effects one-key notches for harshness ("I've reassigned my keyboard shortcuts one through five... if there's a s... condensed harsh waveform... with one click I've got a filter going on at the top, a little dip at 8K a little dip at 5K"). [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-young-thug-gunna-ski), [Sweetwater masterclass clip](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F0Nq34U7Ffs)
- Preemptively EQs hats even in the tracking session to avoid headphone bleed. [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-young-thug-gunna-ski)
- Vocal up-front recipe: "You have to carve out a lot of spaces to fit the vocal... All of the carving usually happens from 300-4k" — and uses Wavesfactory Trackspacer to duck instruments against the vocal. [Matt Rad #77](https://www.livewithmattrad.com/episodes/77)

## Signature Moves
1. **Vox Throw "special sauce" tracks** — 100%-wet printed effect audio dropped on single words; the last mix step; makes rap vocals "radio friendly." [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-young-thug-gunna-ski)
2. **Sidechain-ducked quarter-note delay** (RCompressor keyed to vocal) — delay audible only in vocal gaps. [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-young-thug-gunna-ski)
3. **Acustica Ruby 2 (Fearn) + Ultra Marine 4 (Fairchild) as the tonal "secret weapon"** on every printed vocal, plus Neutron Exciter cranked. [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-young-thug-gunna-ski), [Matt Rad #51](https://www.livewithmattrad.com/episodes/51)
4. **808s: 1–2 parallel chains (Little Labs VOG), clip fades to duck the 808 away from the kick, per-note saturation automation, phase checks** — "I work off of the 808 always." [Matt Rad #51](https://www.livewithmattrad.com/episodes/51)
5. **Clean-up-first workflow:** RX + Clip Effects + Stream Deck macros — surgical removal of harshness/plosives before any tone shaping. [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-young-thug-gunna-ski)
6. **Loudness discipline:** limiter ≤1dB GR, clipping via Burl A/D or T-Racks Clipper (and Mike Dean's Gainstation), "Mixing is really about evening things out so that they work well together." [Matt Rad #77](https://www.livewithmattrad.com/episodes/77)

## What To Steal
1. Auto-Tune FIRST in the chain (before EQ/compression) — exactly how the records are built: Tune → clean-up (de-esser, resonance control) → compression → color.
2. Two-stage vocal processing: surgical clean-up on the track (de-ess, tame harshness — free: TDR Nova dynamic EQ as a Soothe-lite) then tone-shaping on a vocal bus (saturation, opto comp, gentle dynamic EQ).
3. "Vocal throws" concept in any DAW: keep 3–5 100%-wet effect tracks (pitch-shift delay, wide chorus, lo-fi verb) and drop duplicated words onto them for one-word ear candy.
4. Sidechain a delay to the vocal so it only rings in gaps (Audacity can't sidechain natively, but you can duck manually or use a free sidechain-capable VST host plugin).
5. Exciter/presence on the printed vocal (Neutron Exciter concept; free: Fresh Air-style air or stock high-shelf after saturation).
6. Carve 300 Hz–4 kHz on instruments to make room for the vocal; consider Trackspacer-style dynamic ducking (free alternatives exist).
7. 808 discipline: keep the original intact, add parallel sub (VOG concept), clip-fade 808 tails before the kick, automate saturation on low notes.
8. Never let the limiter work harder than ~1dB — get loud from arrangement and balance, not limiting.
