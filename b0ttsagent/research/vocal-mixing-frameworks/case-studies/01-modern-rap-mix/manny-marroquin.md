## Vocal Philosophy
- Hybrid analog/digital, ears over numbers: *"I don't like mixing in the box. I prefer a hybrid of analogue and digital, because I just don't get the same depth of sound and feel in the box."* [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-manny-marroquin)
- The vocal must carry the song: mixing 'Stronger' he *"wanted Kanye's vocal and the lyrics to be the focal point, and for the sample not to get in the way."* [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-manny-marroquin)
- Emotion/contrast over perfection: *"It doesn't really matter what it sounds like. What matters is: Does it work in the song?"* — verse dry, chorus washy, back to dry = controlling the listener's mind. [Audiofanzine](https://en.audiofanzine.com/sound-technique/editorial/articles/leave-it-to-manny.html)
- Don't fight the artist's world: *"To me, it's pointless to start from scratch... I'll keep everything, but I will go through every plug-in that they put on there... Is this a production call, or is this a sonic-improvement call?"* [Audiofanzine](https://en.audiofanzine.com/sound-technique/editorial/articles/leave-it-to-manny.html)

## The Vocal Chain (as documented)
**Kanye 'Stronger' lead vocal chain (with exact settings, SoS 2007):**
1. SSL fader with **high-pass filtering at 160Hz and 200Hz** on the SSL channel.
2. Vocal bus insert: **Neve 32264 compressor, 3:1 ratio, fast recovery** → second fader with **Tube-Tech CL1B, 4:1, medium attack** → **Avalon 2055 EQ** (sizzle at 25kHz, cut at 220Hz) → **Dbx 902 de-esser**. *"There are two compressors because I must have been looking for a speed of attack that the CL1 doesn't have."* [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-manny-marroquin)
3. Section FX: **Line 6 Echo Farm** 577ms delays on words ("more", "haters"); **PCM42 slap delays ~80ms** mixed far back for depth; **AMS delay harmonizer** on hooks; **Amp Farm** distortion for the "I need you right now" section ("crunchy, distorted, mid-rangey"); hook chain: Avalon 2055 + **Summit DCL200** + second Dbx 902. [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-manny-marroquin)
4. **SSL sidechain de-essing (his famous signature):** route vocal to two adjacent SSL channels; channel 1 = sidechain with HPF, extreme narrow **+12dB boost at 6–7kHz** (where sibilance lives), off the mix bus; linked so channel 2's compressor ducks exactly those frequencies. *"There's no de-esser that sounds better than this... it retains the personality of the singer."* (Learned from Barney Perkins.) [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-manny-marroquin)
5. **Mix bus:** **Manley Passive EQ −2–3dB at 3kHz** across the stereo bus ("I prefer Kanye's work to sound round and musical and warm"); SSL bus compression; very minimal ITB bus processing ("Maybe an EQ... to add some air"). [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-manny-marroquin), [Audiofanzine](https://en.audiofanzine.com/sound-technique/editorial/articles/leave-it-to-manny.html)

**Framework (repeatable, documented):**
1. Session prepped by assistant, Pro Tools "treated like a tape machine"; listens to the rough as a blueprint.
2. Starts with the rhythm section, references the vocals every 20–30 minutes.
3. **Faders literally down at zero, builds the mix from scratch** while referencing the rough ("Anytime I get lost, I'll go and listen to the rough").
4. **Stems printing** for near-perfect recall: prints stems with all analog processing, then can finish in the box; "my stems come back in sounding 98% like the mix."
5. Reverb = preset surfing ("I'd rather go through 20 different presets... the one that's closest to what I hear in my head"); depth = reverb tails, height = level, width as an emotional tool only.
6. Mix is done when the "flags" checklist is empty ("By the time you've got no flags left, that's when I'm done with the mix").
7. Monitors: Augspurgers (mains) → KRK E8s (mids) → NS10s (final EQ checks) — "I prefer to mix urban music... on the Augspurgers... then I'll go to the NS10s to make sure that it sounds good on them as well." [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-manny-marroquin), [Audiofanzine](https://en.audiofanzine.com/sound-technique/editorial/articles/leave-it-to-manny.html)

**Signature plugin set (Waves Manny Marroquin Signature Series):** six plugins built around his chains (EQ with vintage freq curves from his room, Triple D de-esser, Tone Shaper for parallel-compression-style richness, Distortion, Delay, Reverb) — his own published framework. [Waves](https://www.waves.com/bundles/manny-marroquin-signature-series), [SoS review](https://www.soundonsound.com/reviews/waves-manny-marroquin-collection)

## Recording / Tracking Side
- Mixer only (doesn't record); requests stems + sessions; works at Larrabee on an 80-input SSL 9000K; hybrid: everything touches analog, prints stems with analog processing. [Audiofanzine](https://en.audiofanzine.com/sound-technique/editorial/articles/leave-it-to-manny.html)
- On 'Stronger': Kanye changed things constantly; "you have to be prepared to make changes on cue. Saying 'Oh, give me two hours to recall that,' does not exist in his world." [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-manny-marroquin)

## Signature Moves
1. **SSL sidechain de-esser** (boost what you want to remove on a linked sidechain channel — the Barney Perkins trick). [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-manny-marroquin)
2. **Dual-compressor vocal stage** (fast comp + CL1B smooth comp) for attack control without pumping. [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-manny-marroquin)
3. **Stems-with-analog-printed recall system** ("coloring book" — you can only add, but you keep the original). [Audiofanzine](https://en.audiofanzine.com/sound-technique/editorial/articles/leave-it-to-manny.html)
4. **"Production call vs sonic-improvement call"** triage of incoming plugins — keep creative FX, replace sonic EQ/comp with his own. [Audiofanzine](https://en.audiofanzine.com/sound-technique/editorial/articles/leave-it-to-manny.html)
5. **−2–3dB at 3kHz on the 2-bus** for warmth/roundness in dense urban tracks. [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-manny-marroquin)

## What To Steal
1. The sidechain de-esser: any DAW with a sidechain-capable compressor can do this — duplicate the vocal, boost 6–7kHz +12dB narrow on the duplicate, use it to key a compressor on the real vocal. Audacity workaround: a free de-esser VST or dynamic EQ (TDR Nova free) with a 6–7kHz band.
2. Two-stage vocal compression: one fast comp (1176-style) then one smooth opto (CL1B-style) — free: TDR Kotelnikov + any 1176-style free comp, or stock comps with different attack settings.
3. HPF the vocal at 160–200Hz and cut ~220Hz — clean low mids before anything else.
4. Air shelf at the very top (Avalon 25kHz "sizzle" concept; stock high-shelf at 12–16kHz also works) after the cuts.
5. Keep the artist's creative FX (vocal throws, filters), replace sonic EQ/comp — triage every plugin in the session.
6. Rebuild the mix from faders down at zero while referencing the rough — you'll hear the real balance instead of inheriting one.
7. Bus dip of 2–3dB around 3kHz for warmth when things get dense.
8. Print stems with processing for easy revisions ("coloring book" recall).
