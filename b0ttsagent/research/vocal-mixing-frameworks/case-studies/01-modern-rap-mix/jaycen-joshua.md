## Vocal Philosophy
- You are hired for taste, not fixes: *"Once you get to the upper echelons of mix engineers, you are being paid for your taste. You're not hired just to make the rough mix sound better... my idea of mixing is to make listening to a record like a movie experience."* [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-jaycen-joshua)
- Respect the rough/demo culture: *"You really have to engulf yourself into these rough mixes nowadays, because these kids spend so much time and so much creative energy on these roughs that if you don't really understand the track and their ideas, you are going to get killed."* [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-jaycen-joshua)
- Tuning is mandatory in modern records, but Auto-Tune is an effect, Melodyne is the tuner: *"I prefer to use Melodyne. I am not a huge fan of Auto-Tune, unless it is the sound that you are looking for. For me it's an effect, not really a tuner."* [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-jaycen-joshua)
- Rap vocals and reverb don't mix: *"'RapVerb' isn't really reverb, because everyone knows that reverb is the kiss of death on rap vocals. Reverb and rap don't mix."* [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-jaycen-joshua)

## The Vocal Chain (as documented)
**Current-era chain (MWTM "Deconstructing a Mix #51" — Lady London "D-R-U-N-K", 2023; plugin lists from the session pages):**
- Lead vocal chain: **Acustica El Rey** (compressor) → **FabFilter Pro-Q 3** → **McDSP MC404** (multiband) → **Fresh Air** (air/presence) → **DeEsser** → **CLA Vocals** (Waves) → **Waves MV2** (leveler). [MWTM part 1](https://mixwiththemasters.com/videos/jaycen-joshua-lady-london-d-r-u-n-k/part/1)
- Rap vocal chain (same session): **DeEsser → CLA Vocals → MV2 → Pro-Q3 → MC404 → AirEQ → CLA Vocals → C6**. [MWTM part 2](https://mixwiththemasters.com/videos/jaycen-joshua-lady-london-d-r-u-n-k/part/2)
- Backing/ad-lib vocals: **Pro-Q3, MV2, CLA Vocals, Soothe2, RCompressor, Waves Center**; vocal FX: **MC404, PS22 XSplit, MV2, TrueVerb, Auto-Tune, AIR Reverb, Spectre**. [MWTM](https://mixwiththemasters.com/videos/jaycen-joshua-lady-london-d-r-u-n-k)
- He confirms he still leans on his **2021 chain** and has a newer 2024 chain; his method is to put the whole chain on **with no settings first**, listen, then dial everything in. (MWTM community Q&A; a 2025 walkthrough of his update notes El Rey swapped for **Waves MV2**.) [MWTM community](https://community.mwtm.com/t/vocal-chain-currently-used/7380), [YouTube 2025 chain walkthrough (3rd-party)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WVK35kiqrMU)

**Documented vocal chain — Bieber "Baby" (2009, Sound On Sound, full detail):**
- **Tuning: Melodyne** (natural tuning; keeps original vocal in playlist for retunes).
- Lead vocal split into multiple tracks, **different EQ/compression per song section** ("When he changes his tone, the EQ has to change").
- Effects 100% in the box on sends: **Digidesign TL Space** convolution reverbs ('Silky Gold Plate', 'QRS', 'Big Pan Hall'), **Massenburg EQ** (tight-Q notches, sweep-solo), **Cranesong Phoenix** ('Luster' — fullness/warmth for harsh young mids), **Waves Renaissance Compressor**, **SoundToys PitchBlender** (ad-libs), **Digi Extra Long Delay** ping-pong ('PingPong'), Waves Enigma, SPX90-style 'RapH3K' patch, 'RapVerb' (delay-based, not reverb), **Waves Center** on backing vocals ('Less Vocal' preset — clears the centre for the lead). [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-jaycen-joshua)
- **Parallel compression philosophy:** kick = **Dbx 160x → Pultec EQP-1A** on a parallel channel (the "Bob Powers" chain he got from Pensado — "It gives you absolutely amazing low end. It's unstoppable"); snare = **160x → API 550A**, parallel; *"If you don't use parallel compression you risk things sounding thin."* [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-jaycen-joshua)
- **Mix bus:** SSL G384 bus comp + GML 8200 + Avalon 2055; three submix groups (rhythm, music, vocals) to six faders before stereo. [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-jaycen-joshua)

**Framework (repeatable, documented):**
1. Demands rough mix + all files 3 days in advance ("I don't work on records for which I don't have all the files, including the rough mix, at least three days in advance"); assistants color-code and label everything.
2. Day of mix: listens to the rough on repeat in the morning; if the rough has something incredible, starts from there.
3. *"When I begin a mix, I'll basically start with attacking what I think is the most important part of the record."* — rhythm section, vocal, or melody.
4. Nulls all board faders at start; never pans in the box; trims on the board.
5. A record a day on average. [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-jaycen-joshua)

## Recording / Tracking Side
- Runs Pro Tools outputs through Class-A **Neve 1073/1084 preamps** into the SSL insert returns on percussive instruments for "roundness and punch" (18 Neves in the room); drums dumped to 2-inch Studer A827 tape when it fits the record ("Colour is very important"). [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-jaycen-joshua)

## Signature Moves
1. **Parallel low-end chains** (Dbx 160x → Pultec on kick/808s; 160x → API 550A on snare) — blend, don't replace. [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-jaycen-joshua)
2. **Melodyne-first, Auto-Tune-as-effect** tuning doctrine. [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-jaycen-joshua)
3. **No reverb on rap vocals — delays and spatial tricks instead** ('RapVerb' = delay; ping-pong delays, SPX90 patches). [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-jaycen-joshua)
4. **Section-split vocal EQ** (different vocal chains per verse/hook). [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-jaycen-joshua)
5. **Chain-on-with-no-settings, then dial** — plus a strict rough-mix-reference discipline (3-day rule). [MWTM community](https://community.mwtm.com/t/vocal-chain-currently-used/7380), [SoS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/secrets-mix-engineers-jaycen-joshua)

## What To Steal
1. Use a real tuner (Melodyne-style, invisible) and treat Auto-Tune strictly as FX when you want the sound — decide which you're doing per song.
2. Rap vocals: skip reverb; use delays (ping-pong, dotted) + a subtle "room" send at most — this alone modernizes a rap vocal.
3. Parallel compression: duplicate the vocal/drums bus, smash it (fast attack, high ratio), blend underneath — the modern "expensive" glue. Free: stock compressors on a bus.
4. Split your vocal chain by section if the song changes character (verse vs hook EQ/comp).
5. Chain discipline: insert the whole chain with defaults, listen, then dial — avoids rabbit-holing on one plugin.
6. Reference the rough constantly (put it in the session, A/B) — and if you record over MP3s, do the same: keep the original beat file in the session as reference.
7. Bus glue: SSL-style bus comp + gentle width; keep the low end controlled with a multiband on the 808 bus rather than EQ-only.
