# Rap Vocal Recording Chains (Documented: Drake, Kanye, Young Thug, Travis Scott, Meek Mill)

- **Lane:** Recording / Tracking — full documented recording chains and workflows for mainstream rap vocals, 2018–2025 era records with older foundational interviews as evidence
- **Anchored in:** Noah "40" Shebib & Noel Cadastre (Drake), Anthony Kilhoffer & Mike Dean (Kanye West), Bainz & Flo Ongonga (Young Thug/Gunna), Ca$hpassion (Travis Scott), Anthony Cruz (Meek Mill), Thomas "Tillie" Mann (Lil Baby/Migos)
- **Evidence base:** A-tier: Sound On Sound (Inside Track: 'Ski'; Recording Drake's 'Headlines'), TapeOp #122, Mix With The Masters (#142 Runaway). B-tier: Mixonline (Take Care), Billboard (Ca$hpassion), Produce Like A Pro (Cruz), Live with Matt Rad Ep. 51, Tube-Tech official. C-tier: Equipboard and blog writeups (explicitly labeled — notably the "Cadastre chain" below).

## 1. DRAKE — the OVO chain (Noah "40" Shebib; engineer Noel Cadastre)

**Proof of legitimacy:** 40 produced/engineered Drake's So Far Gone–era work through Certified Lover Boy (2021); Cadastre has engineering credits on every Drake album from Thank Me Later (2010) through Scary Hours 2 (2021) and was executive producer on For All the Dogs (2023). [Mississauga Music Walk of Fame, https://musicwalkoffame.ca/inductees/noel-cadastre/]; [Metalworks Institute, https://metalworksinstitute.com/mwi-alumni-unleash-on-for-all-the-dogs-album/]

**The documented chain (40's own words, SOS):**
1. Mic: Sony C-800G (or Neumann U87; or Shure SM57 when Drake works in the control room) [SOS, https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/noah-40-shebib-recording-drakes-headlines]
2. Preamp: Neve 1073 or 1081 [SOS, ibid.]
3. Compressor: Teletronix LA-2A — "not hitting that too hard" [SOS, ibid.]; Mixonline confirms: "Shebib also places an LA-2 compressor before Drake's vocal, but touches it very lightly." [https://www.mixonline.com/recording/drake-take-care-366321]
4. Recorded into Pro Tools; vocal chain continued in the box with Antares Auto-Tune, Waves Q8/Q10, de-esser, RE6/Renaissance EQ, RVox, Bomb Factory Pultec EQP-1A, Avid Smack! (documented on 'Headlines') [SOS, https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/noah-40-shebib-recording-drakes-headlines]

**Recording workflow (40, verbatim-documented):** "I arm one track in Pro Tools to record him, and I then drag the clip down to an empty track. I never switch tracks while recording… I always have an open mic in the studio." Drake doesn't want punch-in pre-roll: "Drake doesn't like that, he wants to hear himself live at all times, just with a bit of reverb or delay for the feel." [SOS, ibid.]

**The "Cadastre chain" (C-tier, flagged):** The often-quoted modern Drake chain — C-800G → Neve 1073 → LA-2A → 1176 — is repeated across Equipboard and studio blogs but I could not verify a verbatim Cadastre statement of it in any accessible primary interview. Treat the *specific* 1176 stage as unverified; the C-800G/1073/LA-2A core is A-tier via 40. [Equipboard, https://equipboard.com/pros/drake — C-tier]; [blog example, https://blog.imseankim.com/hip-hop-vocal-mixing-chain-processing-recording-to-master-guide/ — C-tier]

**Mobile rig (why this matters for home recordists):** 40 on the road with Drake: "a little rackmount on wheels that has an Omni HD interface in it and a Neve 1073 strip and a one-unit Magma chassis with a Native card. I plug this into my laptop wherever I go." [Mixonline, https://www.mixonline.com/recording/drake-take-care-366321] — the whole hit chain fits in a rack on wheels.

## 2. KANYE WEST — the documented vocal chain (Kilhoffer / Mike Dean)

**Proof of legitimacy:** Mike Dean has mixed/produced for Kanye since College Dropout; Kilhoffer engineered MBDTF-era records ("Runaway," "All of the Lights"); Tube-Tech's feature documents CL-1B use on Yeezus (2013) and live shows. Mike Dean mixed/co-produced Donda (2021) and Travis Scott's Utopia (2023). [TapeOp, https://tapeop.com/interviews/122/mike-dean]; [Billboard 2023 feature, https://www.billboard.com/music/features/mike-dean-producer-kanye-west-the-idol-synths-interview-1235433506/]

**The documented chain (MWTM #142, Kilhoffer's 'Runaway' session):**
- Tracking: Telefunken ELA M 251 and/or Sony C-800G → Neve 1073 → Tube-Tech CL-1B
- Lead vocal processing: Auto-Tune Pro, Waves C1 Gate, Avid SansAmp PSA-1, McDSP Filter Bank E606 EQ, Avid ff d2 6-band, D-Verb, Space, Soundtoys EchoBoy
- MWTM commenters on the session: "Shocked how little processing is on Runaway… Just the sound of ELA 251, 1073 and CL1B can be amazing." [https://mixwiththemasters.com/videos/jeff-bhasker-anthony-kilhoffer-kanye-west-runaway]

**CL-1B is contract-level for Kanye:** "TUBE-TECH used exclusively for all Kanye West vocals on his 2013 album 'Yeezus'… Kanye West doesn't perform without his CL 1B in the vocal chain, be it live or in the studio." (His sound engineer: Noah Goldstein.) [Tube-Tech, https://www.tube-tech.com/cl1b-is-the-one-and-only-for-kanye-west/]

**Auto-Tune is part of the tracking sound:** On 808s & Heartbreak Mike Dean confirmed to MTV: "[Kanye] fell in love with the Auto-Tune," and imposed a rule for the record: "Every song has got to have an 808 in it." [MusicRadar, https://www.musicradar.com/news/tech/kanye-west-loves-auto-tune-808-sounds-and-naked-girls-177589] Auto-Tune Pro appears in Kilhoffer's documented tracking/processing chain for Runaway. [MWTM, ibid.]

## 3. YOUNG THUG & GUNNA — the Bainz template (YSL)

**Proof of legitimacy:** Bainz has engineered/mixed for YSL since 2017: Slime Language (2018), So Much Fun (2019, US #1), Slime Language 2 (2021, US #1), Punk (2021); MWTM produced a Bainz mixing series. [SOS Inside Track, https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-young-thug-gunna-ski]

**The chains (verbatim):**
- Atlanta: "A lot of the vocal recordings were done in the SSL room using a Sony C800G, Neve 1073, dbx 902 de-esser and a Summit Audio TLA." [SOS, ibid.]
- LA: "In LA our vocal chain is C800, 1073 and then a Tube-Tech CL-1B." [SOS, ibid.]
- Plugin detail (Live with Matt Rad): two de-essers — "One cutting 200-250, one cutting a little 4k"; "Lots of clip-gaining and clip effects"; subtle cuts only ("Everything he's done in the chain up to this point is cutting"). [https://www.livewithmattrad.com/episodes/51]

**The recording workflow (the repeatable framework):**
1. "The beat is loaded as a 2-track into my Pro Tools recording template, and they start improvising over it." [SOS, ibid.]
2. Rappers work out verses in the control room, surrounded by people — "Sometimes the entire vocal is laid down in less than an hour." [SOS, ibid.]
3. Template: "It's got two record tracks, with Antares AutoTune, a playlist track, a bunch of vocal audio tracks, and a dozen aux effects tracks" — "built for speed and low latency." [SOS, ibid.]
4. The artist comps: "Thug is his own vocal producer. He will pick the takes, or even the bars… sometimes he gets on the computer and does the comps himself." [SOS, ibid.]
5. Timing is nudged deliberately: "Both Thug and Gunna listen back for timing a lot… Almost every part gets nudged around… for the most part we're nudging vocals back a little bit, because it's the feel they want." (Contrast: DaBaby "slightly ahead of the beat.") [SOS, ibid.]
6. Because takes happen in a noisy control room: "there'll be a lot of clean-up… using iZotope RX." [SOS, ibid.]

**Proof of legitimacy:** Astroworld (2018, Billboard 200 #1), Utopia (2023, #1); mixed in large part by Mike Dean; tracked by Ca$hpassion since 2011. ## The Repeatable Pattern Across All Five Artists

1. **Record to the 2-track beat** (no stems needed to cut vocals). [Bainz, SOS]
2. **One vocal chain, always the same:** bright tube condenser → Neve-class preamp (1073/1081/BAE 1073) → tube/optical compressor (CL-1B or LA-2A) → light de-ess. (C-800G + 1073 + CL-1B/LA-2A = the spine of all five documented chains.)
3. **Auto-Tune live in the tracking path** (Kanye, Thug's template, Travis's sound; Drake's raps tuned in the box by 40).
4. **Vibe first, cleanliness later** — record in the room where the artist performs best, fix noise with RX after.
5. **Timing nudges are a creative tool**, not a fix (Thug/Gunna behind-beat feel; DaBaby ahead).
6. **Templates for speed** — Bainz's recording template, Tillie's shared template, 40's drag-and-drop workflow.

## Home Setup Translation

- Your interface preamp ≈ the 1073 role. Add a free/stock Neve-style EQ if you want the "1073 sheen" (e.g., a 3-band with fixed mids at 1.6k/3.2k/4.8k, or free options like the Analog Obsession Neev).
- Replace CL-1B/LA-2A with any stock optical-style compressor, ratio 3:1–4:1, slow attack, gain reduction 3–6 dB max ("not hitting that too hard").
- Record with Auto-Tune-style correction ON while tracking (free: MAutoPitch, GSnap; stock: Logic Pitch Correction / FL Pitcher) if you want the Kanye/Travis/T-Pain "live effect" — but only if you're singing through it on purpose (see tuning-workflows.md).
- Build Bainz's template: 2 record tracks (one with tuning plugin), 1 playlist/comp track, 4–6 vocal layers, 4 aux FX (reverb, delay, de-ess, parallel comp).
- Nudge your takes: rap vocals that sit *slightly behind* the beat read as "cooler" (Thug); *slightly ahead* reads as aggressive (DaBaby).
- Accept the room: sing close on a dynamic if your room rings; clean up mouth/room clicks with a free RX-lite (Audacity's noise reduction, or iZotope RX De-click if you own it).

## What To Steal

1. Pick ONE chain and use it on every song for a year (every pro above has a single documented chain).
2. Record onto the beat's 2-track — it's how every record here was actually cut; stems only matter at mix time.
3. Light compression on the way in (3–6 dB, optical) — 40's "not hitting that too hard."
4. De-ess between preamp and compressor (Bainz) — do it in the box before your compressor insert.
5. Track with Auto-Tune live if your style needs it (Thug's template has it on the record tracks).
6. Don't stop the session for noise — capture the vibe, fix the noise after (Bainz/RX, Tillie/RX).
7. Use clip gain and clip EQ for per-line control (Bainz: "Lots of clip-gaining and clip effects") — this is free in every DAW.
8. Nudge vocals as a feel tool, not a correction tool.
9. Keep the template boring and fast — Bainz: "the template is built for speed and low latency."
10. Your room is fine: 40's road rig was one rack on wheels; Tillie's artists cut hits in hotels and dressing rooms.
