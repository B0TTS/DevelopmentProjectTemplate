## Vocal Philosophy
The Eilish aesthetic is deliberate anti-pop: no high end, no reverb, whisper-close vocals. Kinelski's role is to not break it:
- "When I mix a vocal like that, I take a transparent approach. I don't want to get in the way of the magic. It's all about subtlety." [Waves Q&A](https://www.waves.com/waves-grammys-2021-kinelski-billie-eilish)
- "As you get more experience, you realise you don't need to do as much. Sometimes mixing involves literally doing nothing." [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-billie-eilish-bad-guy)

## The Vocal Chain (as documented)
From the SOS *Inside Track: Billie Eilish 'Bad Guy'* (2019) — the exact chain on all four vocal groups (Lead, LV Doubles, LV Harmonies, Backing Vocals):

1. **FabFilter Pro-Q 2** — "The chain starts with the Pro-Q 2, which is a low cut. That's very standard."
2. **Waves PuigChild 670** — "a compressor I use on all vocals, because I just really like the sound of it. It doesn't hit it very hard, but it smooths the vocals out a bit and adds some colour."
3. **Waves De-Esser** — "set to 6557Hz. It's my standard de-esser, and it catches some post-compression nastiness." (If a vocal is tricky: add FabFilter Pro-DS at the end too.)
4. **FabFilter Pro-Q 2 (2nd)** — "taking out some mids between 200Hz and 2kHz."
5. **UAD Neve 1073 EQ** — "in this particular session is just engaged. I like the colour it adds, so I just throw it on."
6. **Waves Vocal Rider** — "does 1.5dB of treatment. If there are some words that drop out, it brings them up, and vice versa."
7. **Manual rides** on an Avid Artist Mix fader bank, after everything.

All four vocal groups share the same chain; only the EQ varies per group ("I might cut some mid-range out of the doubles, for example"). The identical chain was reused across the whole album with just settings tweaks: "after I had dialled in Billie's vocal chain on the first song... I did not change it any more, but just tweaked the settings."

**Tuning:** none in the mix. "With Billie's vocals, I did not do any timing correction or tuning. As I said earlier, her vocals are fully produced." His cleanup is limited to pencil-tooling pops/clicks that compression exposes: "I don't go too crazy on sibilance and all that stuff, because if it feels good, I do not want to over-doctor it."

**Width:** SoundToys MicroShift via a Width aux on vocals — "If you can hear the effect, it's too much. It's meant to be felt." [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-billie-eilish-bad-guy) [Waves](https://www.waves.com/waves-grammys-2021-kinelski-billie-eilish)

**Master bus (all album songs):** UAD Ampex ATR-102 (tape, later removed at Finneas/Billie's request — hiss ruined the silence) → UAD Thermionic Culture Vulture ("a hair of drive") → FabFilter Pro-L 2 ("EDM-Aggressive and Tight" preset), after hardware summing via Dangerous 2-Bus + Dangerous compressor. [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-billie-eilish-bad-guy)

**Plugin count:** "You can probably count all the plug-ins I use on two hands. I like to keep it simple."

## Recording / Tracking Side
Kinelski does not track Eilish — he receives stems from Finneas: "Finn sends me great stems, with the vocal production already dialled in, including all distortion and other effects... Everything that Finn sent me was super-consistent." His stated mix process is old-school/hip-hop order: kick, snare, hats → vocals with drums → bass → keys; dial the mix-bus compressor early and "mix into that"; leave detailed vocal "surgery" for last ("It's really easy in this day and age to overdo surgery"). [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-billie-eilish-bad-guy)

## Signature Moves
1. **Cut-only EQ, gentle compression** — "With EQ, I usually just cut frequencies and rarely boost. Compression is often gentle." [Waves](https://www.waves.com/waves-grammys-2021-kinelski-billie-eilish)
2. **PuigChild 670 as the color comp** (or R-Vox for tightening on other vocals) — documented on "Bad Guy" and "Everything I Wanted". [Waves](https://www.waves.com/waves-grammys-2021-kinelski-billie-eilish)
3. **De-esser fixed at 6557Hz**, post-compression, as the standard sibilance catch. [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-billie-eilish-bad-guy)
4. **Vocal Rider + hand-written rides** — level consistency via 1.5dB rider, then expressive automation by hand. [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-billie-eilish-bad-guy)
5. **No tuning/timing in the mix** — the mixer trusts the production; the "dark/close/intimate" formula lives upstream. [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-billie-eilish-bad-guy)
6. **Hardware-summed master with tape+tube+limiter** to make ITB mixes "not sound like they were done inside a computer." [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-billie-eilish-bad-guy)

## What To Steal
1. **One vocal chain, reused per project**: HPF → gentle color comp → de-esser → mid cut (200Hz–2kHz) → barely-engaged EQ for color → level rider → hand rides. Every piece maps to stock/free tools (ReaEQ, a free 670-style comp or any smooth optical, Audacity's de-esser or a simple dynamic EQ, ReaFir).
2. **Cut, don't boost** — start from "cut frequencies and rarely boost."
3. **De-ess AFTER compression** at a fixed ~6.5kHz spot — catches what compression creates.
4. **Do your detailed vocal surgery last**, after the balance and vibe are set; solo and fix only what jumps out.
5. **Receive "fully produced" vocals** — if you're the artist: produce the tuning, timing, distortion and FX before the mix, so the mixer's job is balance, not repair.
6. **Keep width effects inaudible** — "it's meant to be felt."
7. **Pencil-tool pops** instead of over-de-essing; quiet singers need noise-floor cleanup after compression.
8. **Mix into your bus compressor** — dial it early, then everything else lands in context.
9. **Make the vocal "super-present" with level consistency** — Vocal Rider-style automation (or careful manual gain envelopes) keeps a whisper-level vocal audible in a loud world.
10. **A tape/saturation + limiter master chain** (free: any tape/saturation plugin + a limiter) — but check with the artist; the hiss was rejected on *WWAFA*.
