## Vocal Philosophy
The Eilish sound is a *recorded* aesthetic — close, quiet, dry, intimate — and Finneas treats the bedroom as an instrument:
"The bedroom has a very specific sound, very tight and intimate and closed and quiet. I love the way it makes vocals sound." [Mixonline](https://www.mixonline.com/recording/finneas-on-producing-billie-eilishs-number-one-album-in-his-bedroom)

"That dryness is all about intimacy. A dry vocal sounds really intimate: it makes it sound as if she's singing right in front of you, and I think that's really exciting. So it was a very conscious decision." [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/people/billie-eilish?page=2)

He explicitly rejects tuning-as-fix: "I don't ever tune her vocals as a fix. If she sings a note that's a little bit off, I don't correct it." [SOS p.2](https://www.soundonsound.com/people/billie-eilish?page=2)

## The Vocal Chain (as documented)
Finneas is a minimal-gear producer; his documented "chain" is mostly tracking + editing + deliberate effects rather than a plugin stack. From SOS (2019) + Mixonline (2019) + MWTM:

**Tracking chain (documented, evolution):**
- DAW: **Logic Pro X** (from age 13). [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/people/billie-eilish)
- "Ocean Eyes" era: **Roland Quad Capture** interface, **Yamaha HS5 + HS8S** monitors, **Audio-Technica AT2020** ($80) microphone. [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/people/billie-eilish)
- Present day (from 2019): **UA Apollo 8** (later Apollo X), **Neumann TLM 103**, Yamaha HS8s, Akai MPK249, Nord Stage 3. "I record everyone with the one microphone... I have never found a microphone that I like more than the 2020 that's less expensive than the Neumann. The 2020 is a really good starter microphone." [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/people/billie-eilish)
- "I always encourage people not to think that they have to spend a lot of money on gear. The less stuff you have, the easier it is to make music." [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/people/billie-eilish)

**Vocal editing (his #1 documented task):**
"The hardest thing I have to do with each song is to cut the background noise. Billie sings so quietly that I have to go in with every vocal take we use, and crossfade into silence between every word she sings, otherwise there'd be a noise floor behind the vocal." [SOS p.2](https://www.soundonsound.com/people/billie-eilish?page=2)

**Vocal effects (documented list):**
- "I use the Logic compressor, and Logic's Vocal Transformer, Pitch Correction and Sample Delay plug-ins, Antares' Harmony Engine, and often Soundtoys' Little AlterBoy."
- **Logic Vocal Transformer** = the 'Bad Guy' "rattlesnake" vocal. [Mixonline](https://www.mixonline.com/recording/finneas-on-producing-billie-eilishs-number-one-album-in-his-bedroom)
- **Harsh tremolo**: "I use really harsh tremolo a lot. It's set to zero percent smooth, so it's chopping the signal, and I'll set it to eighth- or sixteenth-note values, then set the depth." [Mixonline](https://www.mixonline.com/recording/finneas-on-producing-billie-eilishs-number-one-album-in-his-bedroom)
- **Auto-Tune only as an effect**: "We also started experimenting with Antares Auto-Tune. The song 'Listen Before I Go' has Auto-Tune, and some of the vocals on 'Bury A Friend' as well, but it's a very strong effect, like Kanye/T-Pain-type Auto-Tune."
- **Reverb**: "Pretty much every reverb that I use is by Valhalla — the Room, Plate, Shimmer... I use reverbs in very specific places, and the rest of the album is dry."
- **Distortion as a feature**: 'Xanny' chorus vocal intentionally distorted; Kinelski blended a clean take underneath. [SOS p.2](https://www.soundonsound.com/people/billie-eilish?page=2) [SOS Bad Guy](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-billie-eilish-bad-guy)

**Handoff (the documented Finneas→Kinelski contract):**
"Everything that I've sent that he's mixed is pre-compressed and pre-panned, and all the reverbs and all the delays are pretty much already there. I'm not looking for Rob to 'fix' anything; he just makes it a little more balanced, because everything comes out of my bedroom at my parents' house with too much bass." [Mixonline](https://www.mixonline.com/recording/finneas-on-producing-billie-eilishs-number-one-album-in-his-bedroom)

## Recording / Tracking Side
- **Room:** a normal bedroom (later his Los Feliz home studio); no acoustic treatment documented — the room's tight sound is embraced and the noise is fixed in editing ("crossfade into silence between every word").
- **One mic for everyone** — AT2020 → TLM 103.
- **Vocal stacking is arrangement**: "'Bad Guy'... the thing that's interesting isn't necessarily the vocal melody on the verses; it's that there's three-part harmony and it's the rhythm." [Mixonline](https://www.mixonline.com/recording/finneas-on-producing-billie-eilishs-number-one-album-in-his-bedroom)
- **Foley**: self-recorded sounds (Tascam DR-05) as production elements — the "scenes in a movie" approach. [SOS p.2](https://www.soundonsound.com/people/billie-eilish?page=2)
- **MWTM 'Therefore I Am'** Inside the Track documents his vocal production on the 2020 single (comping/stacking/effects). [MWTM teaser](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7Y6aFCS8evg)

## Signature Moves
1. **Record-then-repair-quietly**: whisper-level takes + word-by-word noise cuts — the documented formula for the "close to the mic" Eilish intimacy.
2. **Dry by default, Valhalla only where it counts** — intentional, sparse reverb ("That dryness is all about intimacy").
3. **Pitch tools as color, not correction** — Auto-Tune as T-Pain-style effect; "I don't ever tune her vocals as a fix."
4. **Logic stock plug-ins as creative engines** — Vocal Transformer (rattlesnake), harsh zero-smoothness tremolo, Sample Delay; no expensive chains required. [Mixonline](https://www.mixonline.com/recording/finneas-on-producing-billie-eilishs-number-one-album-in-his-bedroom)
5. **Hand over finished vocal stems** — pre-compressed, pre-panned, FX printed, "too much bass" intentionally left for the mixer/mastering to balance.

## What To Steal
1. **A cheap condenser is enough** — the AT2020 recorded a diamond-certified #1 album; buy one mic and learn it.
2. **Embrace an untreated room's tight sound**; fix the consequences in editing (noise-gate/crossfade silence between words for quiet singers).
3. **Dry vocals read as intimacy** — before adding reverb, ask if the song is better without it (use a free/stock plate sparingly: Audacity's built-in reverb or a Valhalla-class free plate like TAL-Reverb-4... note: use any stock reverb).
4. **Never tune as a fix** — keep natural pitch, or use tuning only where it's an obvious effect; for the dark/intimate lane this is a documented Grammy-winning stance.
5. **Stock plugins for character** — Vocal Transformer/format-shifters (free: MAutoPitch, or any formant shifter) and tremolo at 0% smoothness chopped to 8ths/16ths are the documented 'Bad Guy' vocal weapons.
6. **Deliver finished vocal stems to whoever mixes** — compression, panning, reverb and delays decided in production; "the mixer is not there to fix productions."
7. **Record everyone on the same mic** for a consistent catalog sound.
8. **Layer real harmonies at equal level to the lead** — the Eilish arrangement signature.
9. **Write/record daily with minimal gear, even on tour** — a mic, interface and keyboard is the whole documented studio.
10. **Let the mix/master handle the final balance** — Finneas intentionally sends "too much bass" and lets Kinelski + John Greenham fix translation.
