## Vocal Philosophy
"Many people shit on pop music... I actually think it's the most mysterious and advanced, because you have to make the most amount of people happy. It's like a riddle, and there are no rules." [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-dua-lipa-dont-start-now)

On recording singers:
"When I record someone's vocal, I tell the singer not to worry too much about pitch, but to just give me emotion. I can help with the pitch afterwards, and I'd rather it sounds honest. I'm always going for a delivery that sounds believable, for a story that is being told, rather than someone just reciting lyrics. The latter gives you bad pop music, because you feel disconnected from the artist." [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-dua-lipa-dont-start-now)

## The Vocal Chain (as documented)
The SOS *Inside Track: Dua Lipa 'Don't Start Now'* (May 2020) is one of the most detailed published pop vocal-production chains. Kirkpatrick works in Cubase Pro.

**Lead vocal chain (verbatim order):**
1. **Brainworx BX_cleansweep v2** — "very smoothly removes some very low end, around 30Hz, so the compressor acts more on the lower mids, instead of getting caught up with this sub-audible low end."
2. **Goodhertz Vulf Compressor** — "a pretty insane compressor, but in this case it does not do that much. It is all about trying to make it sound a bit more analogue, so it also adds a little bit of grit."
3. **Waves LA-3A** — "a super-smooth compressor that keeps vocals sounding natural, and adds just a little more compression."
4. **Oeksound Soothe** — "just taking out a little bit of the body of the vocal. Dua has very prominent lower mids in her voice, the baritone aspect, and this can sometimes overpower things."
5. **UAD Pultec EQP-1A** — "taking out a little bit more of the low end, and then there is a boost at 12kHz, which adds some air on the vocal... Sometimes I will boost the highs going into a de-esser, so I did that here."
6. **FabFilter Pro-DS** — de-esser.
7. **FabFilter Pro-Q 2** — "to give back a little of the high end the vocal may have lost in the de-esser."
8. **Xfer LFO Tool** — kick-ducking: "The volume is getting ducked by 10 percent when the kick hits, and in the verses that automation goes to 40-percent ducking. This is the kind of stuff that I told Josh [Gudwin] to watch out for, because he needs to make sure he does not add more compression to my stem, because it takes away the LFO Tool's ducking."
9. **Valhalla VintageVerb** — reverb, with **Xfer OTT** on it for control and a **UAD 1176** compressing the reverb return; the LFO Tool's automation is copied to the reverb so the ducking stays coherent. [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-dua-lipa-dont-start-now)

**Vocal-chop FX chain (bridge, "vocal chop"):**
SoundToys Little AlterBoy (formant down, "makes the 'throat' bigger") → MicroShift (width on a mono vocal) → Waves H-Delay (dotted-eighth, very light) → FabFilter Saturn used as a compressor ("It is like side-chaining your delay... the moment the vocal comes back, it erases the delay") → Waves C1 ("insanely fast attack... eliminating any peaks") → FabFilter Pro-DS → Xfer LFO Tool. [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-dua-lipa-dont-start-now)

**Tuning (the documented workflow):**
"After I finished recording vocals, I comp them without tuning, and then I put everything into Melodyne. I used both Melodyne and Auto–Tune, but working with Melodyne is like playing God. It gives you so much control!" [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-dua-lipa-dont-start-now)

**Comping (his signature 3-pass method):**
"When I comp vocals I comp once, and then I take away everything I comped, and then I comp everything again, and I take that away again, and then I do the same thing again... So I do three comps of the whole song. I then tune and time all those three comps, and then I do the final comp from these three comps. Sometimes you lose perspective when comping vocals for hours, and I want to be able to make the final decision instinctively and really quickly at the very end." [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-dua-lipa-dont-start-now)

**Auto-Tune as a creative instrument (2026):** Kirkpatrick's sessions "run on Auto-Tune — not as a safety net, but as a deliberate part of his sound," using Retune Speed as a creative parameter. [Antares](https://www.antarestech.com/blog/ian-kirkpatrick-autotune-interview)

## Recording / Tracking Side
- **Room/DAW:** home studio in a converted bedroom, Tarzana LA; Cubase Pro + UA Apollo 16; Barefoot MM27s. "I had planned to build a studio in the garage, but old habits die hard, and I prefer the bedroom environment!" [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-dua-lipa-dont-start-now)
- **Mics:** "Dua tends to record at my place, using my Telefunken [ELA M] 251, or sometimes my Shure SM7. Many of the vocals here are recorded on the SM7, handheld."
- **Take management:** "I do of course get vocal recordings and comps made by others, and however great they are, I always ask for the other takes, so I can tweak the vocal production. I like to have options."
- **Division of labor:** he does vocal recording + vocal production himself, sends stems + rough mix to Josh Gudwin for mixing ("I also usually do the vocal recording and production... After I finished recording vocals... I do a detailed rough mix, before sending stems to mixer Josh Gudwin"). [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-dua-lipa-dont-start-now)

## Signature Moves
1. **3-pass comping** — three full comps, all tuned/timed, final comp chosen "instinctively at the very end." [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-dua-lipa-dont-start-now)
2. **Comp→tune→final-comp order** — no tuning until after the raw comp; Melodyne is the tuning tool, Auto-Tune is flavor.
3. **Sidechain-duck the vocal to the kick** with LFO Tool (10% verses / 40% chorus-style) so "the song and the vocal are interacting" — and warn the mixer not to undo it. [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-dua-lipa-dont-start-now)
4. **Saturn-as-delay-ducker** — using a saturator's dynamics section to duck the delay when the vocal re-enters ("like side-chaining your delay"). [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-dua-lipa-dont-start-now)
5. **Pultec-style air-before-de-esser** — boost highs *into* the de-esser, then restore with a clean EQ after. [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-dua-lipa-dont-start-now)
6. **Emotion-over-pitch direction** to singers, then fixing pitch later in Melodyne. [SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-dua-lipa-dont-start-now)

## What To Steal
1. **Comp 3 times, tune all 3, then final-comp** — the single most concrete anti-tunnel-vision workflow in pop vocal production.
2. **HPF ~30Hz before the compressor** so sub-rumble doesn't trigger it (free: any high-pass — Audacity's EQ, ReaEQ).
3. **Order matters: gentle comps → resonance tame → Pultec-style tone → de-esser → restore highs.** The exact order Kirkpatrick documents works with stock/free plugins (ReaComp/LA-3A-style free comps, TDR Nova for Soothe-like dynamic cuts, any pultec-style free EQ).
4. **Duck the vocal to the kick** (sidechain compressor or LFO tool; Audacity: sidechain ducking via automation or a compressor sidechain in a real DAW) — a defining 2020s pop move.
5. **Duck the delay when the vocal returns** — route the send through a compressor keyed to the vocal (or a gate on the return).
6. **Ask singers for emotion, not pitch** — record honest takes, fix later; "bad pop music" comes from disconnected recitals.
7. **Get the other takes even when handed a comp** — re-vocal-produce from the raw material; options beat convenience.
8. **Record on an SM7-style dynamic or a 251-style condenser** depending on the singer; handheld SM7 is a documented Dua Lipa take sound.
9. **Tell your mixer what NOT to touch** (e.g., don't add compression over your ducking) — hand off stems with a brief.
10. **Use Auto-Tune's retune speed as a creative knob**, not just a fixer — the "deliberate part of his sound" per the 2026 Antares feature.
